Training

Team training. One day, on your own work.

For teams of five to twenty who work in email, documents, and spreadsheets all day. Your machines, your templates, the tool your IT already allows. Everyone leaves with something running.

The day

$1,000per person, up to ten

Five-person minimum. Discounts from eleven, quoted per team. Twenty to a room; larger teams run consecutive days.

  • Travel is included. In person anywhere in the country for ten people or more; under ten, in person within driving range of Flagstaff, or remote on your own machines.
  • A thirty-day return session is included.

The program

$1,400per person

The day, then three monthly half-day sessions on your own work.

  • For teams that want it to stick. Each session picks up what broke, what people stopped doing, and the next workflow.

Before the day

  • Send three real documents you produce every week: a quote, a report, a letter. They go on your machines before I arrive.
  • The day runs in the tool your IT already allows: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude.
  • I work on your machines, in your tenant. Nothing leaves your drive. NDA on request.
  • A one-page IT checklist is sent on booking.

Who should be in the room

  • One function per cohort: the leasing team, or accounting, not both. The day works because everyone in the room does the same kind of work.
  • Managers attend with their teams.
  • The written guide is built so anyone who missed the day can follow it.

How the day runs

  • 8:30Setup. Accounts, tools, and each person's pre-work files on their own machine.
  • 9:00What the tool is and isn't, shown on your documents, not slides. What it can't be trusted with. The data rules for your company.
  • 9:45First workflow. Each person builds the document they produce most, from their own template, and finishes it.
  • 11:15Second workflow. The recurring task with data in it: a spreadsheet cleanup, a summary, a batch of replies.
  • 12:30Lunch.
  • 1:15Each person writes their own working instructions: how I do my job, what I check, what's off limits. This is the piece that outlives the day.
  • 2:30Third workflow, or managers only: where it fits in the week, and what changes in the handoffs between people.
  • 3:30Walk the written guide. Agree the thirty-day number. Book the return session.

What each person leaves with

  • A working setup in the tool your IT allows.
  • One finished document, produced from their own template.
  • Their own written working instructions.
  • A written guide for the whole team.

After the day

Before the day we agree on one number: documents produced a week, or hours on a named task. At thirty days we check it, on the return session that's included. If the number didn't move, that session is where we find out why.
