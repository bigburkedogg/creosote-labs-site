Training

Team training. One day, on your own work.

For teams of five to twenty who work in email, documents, and spreadsheets all day. It runs on your machines, with your templates, in the tool you already allow. Everyone leaves with something running.

The day

$1,000per person, up to ten

Five-person minimum. Five people is $5,000; ten is $10,000. Eleven or more is one quoted number for the team, sent before you book. Twenty to a room; larger teams run consecutive days, each day its own group and its own headcount.

  • Travel is included. In person anywhere in the country for ten people or more; under ten, in person within driving range of Flagstaff, or remote on your own machines at the same price.
  • The room and lunch are yours.
  • A thirty-day return session is included, remote or on site.

The program

$1,400per person

The day, then three monthly half-day sessions on your own work, remote or on site, travel included. Same minimum and same discounts from eleven as the day.

  • For teams that want it to stick. Each session picks up what broke, what people stopped doing, and the next workflow.

Before the day

  • Choose three real documents you produce every week: a quote, a report, a letter. Put them in a folder on your own drive before the day. I don't need copies sent to me.
  • The day runs in the tool you already allow: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude. Everyone signs in with a company account; no personal accounts. Licenses for the tool are yours and are not included in the price. The IT checklist says which plan each tool needs.
  • I work on your machines, inside your own company accounts, and take no copies. The only thing that leaves your drive is what the tool you approved already sends to its vendor. An NDA is available on request, on your paper or mine.
  • A one-page IT checklist is available before you book; ask for it on the call. It covers accounts, which plan each tool needs, what I bring, and what stays on your drive. Accounts are set up by your IT before the day.

Who should be in the room

  • One department per day: the leasing team or accounting, not both. The day works because everyone in the room does the same kind of work.
  • Managers attend with their teams and count in the headcount.
  • Headcount is set when we book. Someone who can't make the day is still counted, can follow the written guide, and can join the return session. The day is not recorded unless you ask; a recording is yours.

How the day runs

  • 8:30Setup. Checking that accounts, tools, and each person's pre-work files work on their own machine. Nothing is installed on staff machines unless your IT chooses a desktop version. I bring my own laptop; it does not join your network.
  • 9:00What the tool is and isn't, shown on your documents, not slides. What it can't be trusted with. The data rules for your company.
  • 9:45First workflow. Each person builds the document they produce most, from their own template, and finishes it.
  • 11:15Second workflow. The recurring task with data in it: a spreadsheet cleanup, a summary, a batch of replies.
  • 12:30Lunch.
  • 1:15Each person writes their own working instructions: how they do their job, what they check, what's off limits. This is the piece that outlives the day.
  • 2:30Third workflow, or managers only: where it fits in the week, and what changes in the handoffs between people.
  • 3:30Walk through the written guide. Agree on the thirty-day number. Book the return session.

What each person leaves with

  • A working setup in the tool you allow.
  • One finished document, produced from their own template.
  • Their own written working instructions, saved on your own drive.
  • A written guide for the whole team.

After the day

Before the day we agree on one number: documents produced per week, or hours spent on one named task. At thirty days we check it, on the return session that's included. If the number didn't move, that session is where we find out why. There is no refund tied to the number; the return session is the remedy. Invoiced on booking, due before the day. Reschedule terms.
