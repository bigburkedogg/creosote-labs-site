Client work

Canyon REO, pending consent to be named

The client is a Grand Canyon river outfitter, thirty years old, about sixty trips a year. The office ran on Excel, OneDrive, HubSpot, QuickBooks Desktop, and paper.

Retired: the four menu attachments that went out with every trip confirmation, the group information sheet, and the five places a trip's details used to live.

Built: a meal-planning app that trip clients use to plan and submit their menus, a rebuilt trip tracker with a due date and a done date for every milestone of every trip, and the staff reference and training guides. link to the live app, pending consent

Handed over on the office machine: Claude installed, a reference folder built from their own files, and invoices generated from their own templates.
