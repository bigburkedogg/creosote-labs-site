Insurance agencies in Flagstaff: AI, systems, and websites

Independent insurance agencies in Flagstaff commonly manage policies in an agency management system such as Applied Epic, HawkSoft, AMS360, or EZLynx, quote personal lines through a comparative rater, and handle certificates of insurance, renewal notices, and claims paperwork by email. Prospect follow-up and renewal outreach often live in a spreadsheet or the producer's inbox, and ACORD applications are frequently retyped from information the client already gave once by phone.

Creosote Labs, based in Flagstaff, starts with a one-day operations review of the agency's quoting, renewal, and service workflows and delivers a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. From there we build the pieces the agency chooses: a renewal pipeline that pulls from the management system, automated certificate requests, a quote request form on the website, and an AI setup that drafts coverage summaries and client emails from the agency's templates.

Common projects

  • A renewal tracker that lists every policy coming due in the next 90 days, with outreach status and remarketing notes for each account.
  • An online certificate of insurance request form that captures the holder's details and routes them to the service team.
  • An intake form that collects the information for a commercial application once and fills the ACORD fields from it.
  • A prospect follow-up automation that sends quote reminders and logs replies to the agency management system.
  • An agency website that lists lines of coverage, carriers represented, and a quote request form for each line.
  • Team AI training in which producers and CSRs use Copilot or Claude to compare policy language and draft coverage explanations.
