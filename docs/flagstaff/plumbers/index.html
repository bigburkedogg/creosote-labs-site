Plumbing companies in Flagstaff: AI, systems, and websites

Many plumbing companies run on a field service platform such as ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber for dispatch and invoicing, QuickBooks for the books, and a mix of text messages and paper for everything in between. Estimates, work orders, permit paperwork, and parts lists often live in separate places, and a job that starts as a phone call can pass through 3 or 4 systems before it is invoiced and paid.

Creosote Labs is based in Flagstaff and works with plumbing companies across Northern Arizona. The work covers all 4 services: an operations review that documents how jobs move from call to payment, systems and automation that connect dispatch, estimating, and accounting, a website with online booking and service pricing, and a one-day AI training session for office staff and technicians using the tools the company already pays for.

Services

  • AI consulting. We assess where AI tools fit in a business, implement them, and train staff to use them in their day-to-day work. Details
  • Systems and automation. We consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into a single system, build the internal tools a business needs, and automate repetitive work. Details
  • Website and web application development. We build websites, customer portals, and web applications for businesses, from a marketing site to a booking or quoting tool customers use directly. Details
  • Team AI training. A one-day, hands-on workshop delivered on a team's own tools, documents, and templates. Every participant leaves with a working setup and a written guide. Details

Common projects

  • An estimate template that builds a priced quote from a technician's site notes and a parts list.
  • An automation that creates a QuickBooks invoice when a job is marked complete in Housecall Pro or Jobber.
  • A website with service pages, emergency contact details, and an online booking form that feeds the dispatch calendar.
  • A maintenance-agreement tracker that schedules water heater flushes and backflow tests and sends customer reminders.
  • A review-request sequence that texts customers after a completed job and routes replies to the office.
  • A one-day workshop where dispatchers and technicians set up AI tools for job notes, customer emails, and permit forms.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do we have to switch off ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro?

    No. Most projects connect the tools a company already uses rather than replacing them. The operations review identifies where a change would help and quotes it separately.

  • What does the operations review cost and what do we get?

    The review is $3,000 for one day on site. It produces a written assessment, prioritized recommendations, and a quote for each item, delivered within the week. The fee is credited toward implementation that begins within 90 days.

  • Can technicians in the field attend AI training?

    Yes. Training is $1,000 per person with a 5-person minimum, and each participant works on their own device with their own job notes and forms. Every participant leaves with a written guide, and the team gets a follow-up session 30 days later.
