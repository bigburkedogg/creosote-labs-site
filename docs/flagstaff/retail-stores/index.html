Retail stores in Flagstaff: AI, systems, and websites

Retail stores in Flagstaff usually run the register on Square, Shopify POS, Lightspeed, or Clover, and keep the books in QuickBooks. What sits outside those systems is the paperwork of buying and stocking: purchase orders, vendor invoices, receiving counts, price changes, and the weekly reorder list, which many stores manage in Excel or on a clipboard. Seasonal swings in foot traffic add a second set of questions about staffing and what to reorder. Our systems, website, and AI services apply to that work.

Creosote Labs is a Flagstaff company, and we start with a day in the store. The operations review documents how product moves from purchase order to shelf to sale, where counts are re-keyed, and which reports the owner builds by hand, then quotes each recommendation. Implementation might be an inventory and reorder tool that reads from the point of sale, an online store connected to the register, or a training day where staff use AI to write product descriptions and vendor emails from the store's own catalog.

Common projects

  • A reorder report that pulls sales from Square or Shopify POS and lists items below their minimum stock level each week.
  • An online store on Shopify that shares inventory with the in-store register so counts stay accurate in both places.
  • A purchase order tool that generates vendor orders from the reorder list and files each confirmation when it arrives.
  • A receiving checklist that reconciles vendor invoices against what was delivered and flags shortages and price changes.
  • An automated follow-up that emails customers a receipt, a gift card balance, or a loyalty reminder after a purchase.
  • A training session where staff write product descriptions, social posts, and vendor emails in Gemini or Copilot from the store's own catalog.
