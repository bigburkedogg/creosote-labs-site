Medical practices in Flagstaff: AI, systems, and websites

Independent medical practices usually run on an electronic health record and billing platform such as athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, or Tebra, alongside a phone system, a fax line, and a clearinghouse for claims. Referrals, prior authorizations, intake paperwork, and lab follow-up frequently happen outside the EHR, tracked in Excel, sticky notes, or a shared inbox. Office managers often spend hours each week reconciling schedules, checking eligibility, and chasing documents from other providers.

Creosote Labs is a Flagstaff-based consultancy that works with medical practices on operations rather than clinical care. The operations review documents the administrative workflow from scheduling through claim submission. Systems and automation projects consolidate the tracking that happens outside the EHR into one place. Website work adds appointment requests, patient forms, and clear information on services and accepted insurance. Team AI training helps administrative staff draft correspondence, summarize documents, and prepare referral packets.

Common projects

  • A referral tracker that logs each outgoing referral and flags the ones with no response after a set number of days.
  • A prior authorization log that records submission dates, payer responses, and next steps in one shared view.
  • A patient-facing website with providers, services, accepted insurance plans, and online appointment requests.
  • An automation that turns completed online intake forms into a summary for the front desk before the visit.
  • A training day for office staff on using AI tools to draft patient letters, appeal denials, and summarize faxed records.

    There is not. Systems, automation, and website projects are quoted at a fixed price from the operations review or a scoping call. Team AI training is $1,000 per person.
