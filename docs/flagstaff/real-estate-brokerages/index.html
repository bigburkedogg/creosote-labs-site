Real estate brokerages in Flagstaff: AI, systems, and websites

Real estate brokerages in Flagstaff often manage transactions in SkySlope or Dotloop, commissions in Brokermint or a spreadsheet, accounting in QuickBooks, and agent recruiting and marketing in a separate CRM. Broker file review, commission disbursement authorizations, agent onboarding packets, and monthly production reports are frequently assembled by an office administrator moving data between these systems. The brokerage's public website is often a franchise template with limited lead capture for individual agents.

Creosote Labs is based in Flagstaff and works with brokerages here and throughout Northern Arizona. A one-day operations review covers how the office handles file compliance, commissions, onboarding, and reporting, and produces a quote for each recommendation. Typical implementation work includes a single transaction and commission tracking system, automated compliance reminders to agents, generated production reports for the broker, and a brokerage website with agent pages and lead routing. Team AI training is delivered on the brokerage's own forms and templates.

Common projects

  • A transaction tracker that pulls contract dates from SkySlope or Dotloop and flags missing documents before broker review.
  • A commission calculator that produces the disbursement authorization and QuickBooks entry from the closed file.
  • An agent onboarding checklist that issues accounts, collects license and E&O documents, and schedules orientation.
  • A monthly production report generated automatically for the broker and each agent from closed and pending files.
  • A brokerage website with individual agent pages, office listings, and a lead form that routes to the correct agent.
  • A one-day training session where agents set up Gemini or Copilot with the office's own listing and buyer templates.
