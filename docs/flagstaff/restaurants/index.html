Restaurants in Flagstaff: AI, systems, and websites

Restaurants in Flagstaff generally run the front of house on a point-of-sale system such as Toast or Square, take reservations through OpenTable, Resy, or the phone, and schedule staff in 7shifts or a shared spreadsheet. Invoices from food and beverage vendors, weekly inventory counts, catering orders, and private event contracts are often handled on paper or in a manager's email, and menu changes have to be updated in the POS, on the website, and on third-party delivery platforms separately.

Creosote Labs is a Flagstaff company and works with restaurants here on site. A one-day operations review covers ordering, inventory, scheduling, catering, and the website, and produces a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Typical implementation connects vendor invoices to a single cost tracker, builds a catering and event request form, and keeps the menu in one place that updates the website and online ordering. Staff training covers using AI for menus, descriptions, and guest correspondence.
