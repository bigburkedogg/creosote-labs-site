General contractors in Flagstaff: AI, systems, and websites

General contractors in Flagstaff commonly estimate in Excel, track jobs in Buildertrend or CoConstruct, keep the books in QuickBooks, and handle subcontractor scheduling and client questions by phone and text. Estimates, proposals, change orders, draw requests, lien waivers, and daily logs are produced for every project, and the numbers in them often live in 3 or 4 places that do not agree by the end of the job. Permit documents and inspection schedules are usually tracked in a separate folder or calendar.

Creosote Labs is based in Flagstaff and works with contractors here and across Northern Arizona. A one-day operations review covers how the company estimates, schedules, bills, and closes out jobs. Typical implementation includes an estimating tool that produces the proposal from a takeoff, change orders that update the contract total and the QuickBooks invoice, job cost reports that run without manual entry, and a website with a project gallery and a quote request form. Office and field staff are trained on the company's own estimates and job files.
