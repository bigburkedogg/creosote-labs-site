Everything Creosote Labs has built, grouped by who it was for. One client is not named until they have agreed to be.

For clients

  • Trip operations for a river outfitter

    In production since 2026 · client

    An outfitter running multi-day river trips kept its trip information in five places and sent four menu-planning attachments with every booking. Creosote replaced that with one system. Guests plan their trip menu in a web planner built on a library of more than 900 recipes; each menu produces a shopping list ordered by store section and a packing list broken out by camp and storage bin, and a dietary restriction entered once reaches every list. A trip tracker records the due date and the done date of each milestone. Creosote also wrote the staff and new-hire guides, recovered three weeks of lost CRM email through the mail and CRM APIs, and set the office up to produce its own invoices from its existing templates.

    The client's name and a reference are available on request.

  • WILD Relating

    Live since October 2026 · client

    A sexology practice needed a way to turn podcast listeners into clients. Creosote rebuilt the practice's site as one application: an eight-question assessment that sorts each visitor into one of five patterns, an email engine that queues the sequence written for that pattern, a shelf of courses with checkout built in, and an admin page that lists every contact and every message. It runs on Render, and the owner edits the content in the browser.

    The assessment is the lead magnet. Checkout and email delivery switch on with the owner's Stripe and sender keys.

    wild-relating.onrender.com

Products

  • Longtable

    In development

    Food planning for events where the headcount and the dietary needs keep changing: plated weddings, retreat and lodge kitchens, backcountry expeditions and week-ahead meal prep. The four formats share one recipe library and one menu generator, so changing a single guest's restriction regenerates the menu, the shopping list, the packing list and the cost per head together.

    The recipe library is Longtable's own.

  • Scenario

    In development

    A planning tool for the money decisions with long effects: selling a rental, refinancing, stopping work for a year. Each option is projected years forward on the same measures, net worth, liquid cash, cash flow and the cash low point, and the options sit side by side.

    The comparable software is sold to financial advisors, not to the person making the decision.

Teaching software

  • The University

    In use since 2026 · private

    A private university for one learner: thirteen courses across history, language, computing, mathematics and the body, twelve of them opening with a placement exam. The modules for a course are written after its placement exam, to the level the learner placed at. A grader checks answers, and review is spaced.

    Runs on Render behind a password.

  • Modern Lyceum

    Live

    A spaced-repetition study application for adults. Material is organized by subject, each session is built from the items due for review, and an item's next review moves further out each time it is answered correctly.

    Installs as a web app and works offline.

    modern-lyceum.onrender.com

    2026

  • Gate City

    In use

  • sigmap

    In use

    Real-estate contract packets arrive as flat PDFs with no fillable fields, so every signature and initial line has to be placed by hand before the packet goes out for signing. sigmap finds those lines from the geometry of the page rather than from an image of it, which gives coordinates exact enough to use.
