Projects

For clients

  • Trip operations for a river outfitter

    In production

    An outfitter running multi-day river trips kept its trip information in five places and sent four menu-planning attachments with every booking. Creosote replaced that with one system. Guests plan their trip menu in a web planner built on a library of more than 900 recipes; each menu produces a shopping list ordered by store section and a packing list broken out by camp and storage bin, and a dietary restriction entered once reaches every list. A trip tracker records the due date and the done date of each milestone. Creosote also wrote the staff and new-hire guides, recovered three weeks of lost CRM email through the mail and CRM APIs, and set the office up to produce its own invoices from its existing templates.

  • WILD Relating

    Live since October 2026

    A sexology practice needed a way to turn podcast listeners into clients. Creosote rebuilt the practice's site as one application: an eight-question assessment that sorts each visitor into one of five patterns, an email engine that queues the sequence written for that pattern, a shelf of courses with checkout built in, and an admin page that lists every contact and every message. It runs on Render, and the owner edits the content in the browser.

  • Scenario

    In development

    A planning tool for the money decisions with long effects: selling a rental, refinancing, stopping work for a year. Each option is projected years forward on the same measures, net worth, liquid cash, cash flow and the cash low point, and the options sit side by side.

    The comparable software is sold to financial advisors, not to the person making the decision.

Teaching software

  • Course builder

    Open source · 13 courses built

    Builds a course on any subject for one learner. It writes the placement exam first: three to six strands, each starting in the middle and moving up or down with every answer, with written questions graded against a rubric, and a result that is a route through the course rather than a score. The modules are then written by a language model to a fixed specification, shorter where the learner placed high and fuller where they placed low, and a checker rejects any quiz question whose answer is not in the module's own text. The finished course runs in a static web app with a quiz engine, written-answer grading and a progress record. Thirteen courses so far, from the history of technology and linear algebra to Spanish and dog training: 92 modules and 12 placement exams. The engine, the checker and the Claude Code skill are open source under MIT.

    github.com/bigburkedogg/course-builder

  • Gate City

    Live

    A reading game for a young reader who reads below grade level, set in an anime dungeon-hunter world. Twelve gates follow the phonics sequence from three-letter words to multisyllable words and affixes, each with word lists, sight words, decodable sentences with comprehension questions and a boss passage. Every word and line is spoken by a neural voice that runs in the browser, every purchase in the shop is a money-math problem, and a save follows the player between devices with a name and a four-number code. The whole game is one HTML file.

    gate-city.onrender.com

  • Gate City: The Mint

    Live

    A math game set in the same world as Gate City. Twelve gates teach the times tables from 2 to 12 and column addition and subtraction, carrying, borrowing and borrowing across zeros, as a mix of the written method and the mental strategy behind it, each with its own visual model. Every problem is read aloud, so a player whose reading lags their math is never stuck on the words. Each solved problem is a strike against a counterfeit coin, and clearing the district rebuilds the Master Die and mints the First Coin.

    gate-city-mint.onrender.com
