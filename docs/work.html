Client

client name, pending consent to be named A river outfitter running approximately 60 trips per year. The office ran on Excel, OneDrive, HubSpot, QuickBooks Desktop, and paper.

Situation

Trip information was maintained in five separate places. Menu planning went out to customers as four attachments with each confirmation. Operating procedures were not documented.

What we did

  • Built a customer-facing meal-planning application that trip clients use to plan and submit their menus, replacing the attachments.
  • Rebuilt the trip tracker with a due date and completion date for every milestone of every trip.
  • Wrote the staff reference guide and new-hire training guide.
  • Set up Claude on the office computer with a reference folder built from the company's own documents, including invoice generation from the company's existing templates.
