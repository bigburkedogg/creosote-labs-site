Website and web application development for marketing agencies

Many marketing agencies build client sites on WordPress, Webflow, or Squarespace, and the agency's own site is often the last project on the board. Agency websites tend to carry a portfolio, a services list, case studies, and a contact form, with new business requests arriving by email and moving into a spreadsheet or HubSpot by hand. Website and web application development covers the agency's own site and the client-facing tools around it.

Creosote Labs builds marketing sites with clear services, pricing where the agency chooses to publish it, and a way to book a call. We also build the tools an agency uses with its clients directly: an intake form that captures a new project's goals, budget, and assets; a client portal where drafts are reviewed and approved; and a reporting page each client can open instead of waiting for a PDF. Hosting, domain configuration, and documentation are part of every project.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • An agency marketing site with case studies, a services page, and a call-booking form that writes leads to HubSpot.
  • A client intake form that collects brand assets, goals, and budget before the kickoff call.
  • A client portal for reviewing and approving creative drafts, with each approval recorded and dated.
  • A live reporting page for each client that reads from Google Analytics and the ad platforms.
  • A quote request tool that gives prospects a scope range based on the services they select.

Frequently asked questions

  • How are website projects priced?

    Each website or web application project is quoted after a scoping call, and the quote is a fixed price for that scope. There is no hourly billing.

  • Can a client portal connect to the tools the agency already uses?

    In most cases, yes. Portals and reporting pages can read from Google Analytics, ad platforms, and project tools that offer an API, and the scoping call confirms which connections are practical.

  • What does the agency need to provide?

    The agency provides copy, logos, and case study material for a marketing site, plus access to any accounts the site or tool connects to. Hosting and domain setup are handled by us, and documentation is handed over at the end.
