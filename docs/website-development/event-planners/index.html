Website and web application development for event and wedding planners

An event planner's website typically carries a portfolio of past weddings and corporate events, a description of planning packages, and a contact form that feeds a client management tool such as HoneyBook or Dubsado. Many planners also rely on The Knot and WeddingWire listings for inquiries, and hand clients a shared spreadsheet or PDF for the guest list, budget, and vendor contacts. Website and web application development covers the public site and the tools clients use during planning.

Creosote Labs builds marketing websites with clear packages, pricing, and a way to book a consultation, and customer-facing tools such as an inquiry form that captures the event date, venue, and guest count before the first call. For planners who want clients working in one place, we build a client portal for timelines, payment schedules, and approvals. Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation are included in every project.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing website with planning packages, portfolio galleries, and a consultation booking form.
  • An inquiry form that checks date availability and collects venue, guest count, and budget range before a call is scheduled.
  • A client portal where each couple or corporate client can view the timeline, approve vendor selections, and see their payment schedule.
  • An online guest list and RSVP tool that the client updates and the planner reads from directly.
  • A vendor directory page that the business maintains in one place and publishes to its site.
