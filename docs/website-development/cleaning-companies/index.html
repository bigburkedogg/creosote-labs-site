Websites and booking tools for cleaning companies

Many cleaning companies find new residential clients through search and referrals, and the first thing a prospective client wants is a price. A typical company website offers a phone number and a contact form, so quoting happens over the phone or after a walkthrough. Commercial prospects want a proposal, references, and proof of insurance. Booking pages bundled with Housecall Pro or Launch27 cover part of this, but the marketing site, the quote form, and the customer account often live in three different places.

Creosote Labs builds the marketing site and the tools behind it as one project. That means service pages with clear pricing by home size or square footage, an instant quote form that calculates a price and books a first clean, a customer portal for viewing upcoming visits, updating entry instructions, and paying invoices, and a commercial page with a proposal request. We set up hosting and the domain, connect the site to the scheduling tool, and hand over documentation.
