Website and web application development for landscaping companies

A landscaping company's website usually has one job: turn a homeowner or property manager who is searching for lawn care, hardscape, or irrigation work into a request for an estimate. Many companies run on a template site that lists services in general terms, has no pricing, and sends contact form messages to an inbox that the crew lead checks between jobs. The scheduling platform, whether Jobber, Service Autopilot, or LMN, often has a booking form of its own that is not connected to the site.

Creosote Labs builds marketing sites for landscaping companies with a clear service list, service area, starting prices where the company wants to publish them, and a quote request form that collects the property address, the service wanted, and photos. We connect that form to the scheduling platform so the request lands as a lead rather than an email. For companies with recurring maintenance clients, we also build customer portals for viewing visit history, approving add-on work, and paying invoices. Hosting, domain setup, and documentation are part of every project.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with separate pages for maintenance, installation, irrigation, and snow removal, each with its own quote request form.
  • A quote request form that asks for the property address and photos and creates a lead in Jobber automatically.
  • A customer portal where maintenance clients see their next scheduled visit, past service notes, and open invoices.
  • An online estimate tool for standard services such as mowing or aeration that gives a price range from lot size.
  • A photo gallery organized by project type that the office can update from a phone after a job is finished.
