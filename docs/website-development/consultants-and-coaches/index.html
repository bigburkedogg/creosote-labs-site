Website and web application development for consultants and coaches

For many consulting and coaching practices the website is where a referral goes to decide whether to book a call. Many practices currently have a Squarespace or Wix site with a services page, a bio, and a Calendly link on the contact page, plus separate tools for an application form, a course login, and a resource library. Prospective clients often move between three or four different domains before they reach the first conversation.

Creosote Labs builds a site that states the practice's services and pricing plainly and gives a visitor one clear way to book or apply. Where the practice needs more than a marketing site, we build the application form, a client portal for session notes and shared documents, or a program page where members log in, as part of the same site. Hosting, domain setup, and handover documentation are included in every project.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with a services page, pricing, practitioner bio, and a booking page connected to the practice's calendar.
  • A client application form that screens prospective clients with the practice's own qualifying questions before a call is offered.
  • A client portal where each client sees their session history, shared notes, and homework in one login.
  • A group program page with member login, a session schedule, and recordings from past sessions.
  • A speaking and workshops page with a request form that collects event date, audience size, and budget.

Frequently asked questions

  • How is a website project priced?

    Website and application projects are quoted after a scoping call. The price is fixed for the agreed scope, with no hourly billing.

  • What does the practice need to provide before the build starts?

    The practice provides a description of each service and its price, a bio, any photos or logo files, and access to the current domain registrar. Where existing copy is unclear, we write it with the owner during scoping.

  • Can the practice keep its current booking and payment tools?

    Yes. Tools such as Calendly, Acuity, Stripe, and QuickBooks are connected to the new site rather than replaced, unless the scoping call identifies a reason to change one.
