Website and web application development in Sedona

Many Sedona businesses find their customers online before those customers arrive in town. Inns and vacation rentals take reservations, tour operators sell seats by the day, spas and studios book appointments, and galleries and shops sell to visitors who want to keep buying after they go home. These businesses often run on a template site plus a separate booking widget, a Shopify or Square store, and a contact form that lands in someone's inbox.

Creosote Labs builds websites and web applications for businesses in Sedona and throughout Northern Arizona. That ranges from a marketing site with clear services, pricing, and a way to book, to customer-facing tools such as quote requests, reservations, intake forms, and portals, to web applications that replace a manual process for staff and customers. Every project is quoted after a scoping call at a fixed price, and hosting setup, domain configuration, and written documentation are part of the handover.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A tour company website with trip pages, availability, and online booking that writes each reservation to the company's calendar.
  • A direct-booking site for a small inn with room pages, rates, and a reservation form, so fewer bookings pay a platform commission.
  • A gallery website with an artist directory and an online store for pieces that ship to buyers after their visit.
  • A retreat or wellness center site with an intake form that collects the information staff need before the first appointment.
  • A quote-request tool for a wedding planner or caterer that turns a form submission into a draft proposal.
  • A staff portal for a vacation rental manager where cleaners and maintenance vendors see their assignments for the day.

  • How is a website project priced?
