Website and web application development for gyms and fitness studios

Gym and studio websites often consist of a template site with a class schedule pulled in from Mindbody or Glofox, a pricing page maintained by hand, and a contact form that delivers to a shared inbox. Membership sign-up and trial booking happen on the platform's hosted pages, which look different from the rest of the site. Waivers are often signed on a tablet at the front desk, and personal training is frequently booked by text message.

Creosote Labs builds marketing sites and customer-facing tools for fitness businesses. A marketing site lists classes, memberships, and pricing plainly, with trial booking and contact on every page. Customer tools include online waiver and intake forms, personal-training booking, and a member portal for package balances and schedule changes. Where the studio wants to replace a platform feature, we build a web application for it. Hosting, domain setup, and handover documentation are included.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with class descriptions, membership tiers, and a trial-class booking form that posts to the membership platform.
  • An online waiver and health-intake form that new members complete before their first visit.
  • A personal-training booking page that shows each trainer's availability and takes a deposit through Square or Stripe.
  • A member portal for viewing package balances, freezing a membership, and updating payment details.
  • A schedule page that reads directly from Mindbody or Mariana Tek so it does not go out of date.

Frequently asked questions

  • How is a website project priced?

    Projects are quoted after a scoping call. The quote is a fixed price for the agreed scope, with no hourly billing. Hosting and domain configuration are part of the project.

  • Can the site connect to our membership software?

    Yes, where the platform offers an integration or an export. Mindbody, Glofox, WellnessLiving, and similar platforms provide schedule widgets or APIs that a site can read from, so bookings and schedules stay in one place.

  • What do we need to provide?

    The project needs current class and membership information, pricing, photos, a logo, and access to the domain registrar and membership platform. Copy is written from that material during the project.
