Websites and client tools for real estate agents

Real estate agents often have a website provided by their brokerage or a template site from a vendor tied to their CRM, with an IDX feed for listings, a contact form, and a page of past sales. Agents also collect information through buyer intake forms, seller questionnaires, open house sign-in sheets, and showing request forms, most of which are handled by paper, email, or generic form builders. Website and web application development covers both the public site and those tools.

Creosote Labs builds marketing websites for agents with clear services, current listings, past sales, and a way to book a consultation directly. We also build customer-facing tools such as a home valuation request, a buyer intake form that feeds the CRM, a client portal that shows where a transaction stands, and an open house sign-in that records visitors. We handle hosting setup and domain configuration and hand over the site with documentation.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A personal agent website with listings, sold properties, service areas, and a consultation booking page.
  • A seller inquiry form that collects property details and photos and returns a request for a market analysis.
  • A client portal where buyers and sellers see upcoming deadlines and the documents still required for their transaction.
  • An open house sign-in page that captures visitor contact details and adds them to the CRM.
  • A single-property landing page template for featured listings with photos, a floor plan, and a showing request.
  • A relocation guide site for agents who serve buyers moving from out of the area.

Frequently asked questions

  • Can the site show MLS listings?

    Yes, where the agent's MLS offers an IDX feed and the brokerage permits it. We configure the feed and confirm the display rules the MLS requires.

  • How is a website project priced?

    Website and application projects are quoted after a scoping call. Pricing is fixed for the agreed scope, with no hourly billing.

  • What does the agent need to supply?

    A logo and brand colors if they exist, headshots and property photos, a list of services and areas served, and the brokerage details required on real estate marketing. We draft the remaining copy with the agent.
