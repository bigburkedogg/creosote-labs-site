Website and web application development for restaurants

A restaurant's website usually has to do a few specific jobs: show the current menu and hours, take reservations through OpenTable, Resy, or Tock, take online orders through Toast, Square, or a third-party app, and collect catering and private-event inquiries. Many restaurants run on a template site from Squarespace or Wix, or on a page the POS vendor provides, with the menu uploaded as a PDF that goes out of date. Online ordering and reservations often sit on separate services with their own branding and fees.

Creosote Labs builds restaurant websites and the customer-facing tools attached to them. A typical project is a marketing site with a menu that staff can edit from a phone, hours and location for search results, and reservation and ordering links wired to the systems the restaurant already uses. Where a template will not do, we build the tool directly, such as a private-dining inquiry form that produces a quote, or a gift card and merchandise store. Hosting, domain setup, and documentation are included.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with a menu editor managers update from a phone, so the printed menu and the web menu match.
  • A private-dining and catering inquiry form that collects date, headcount, and menu choices and returns a quote for the manager to confirm.
  • A direct online ordering page connected to the POS so orders print in the kitchen without a third-party commission.
  • A multi-location site with per-location hours, menus, and Google Business Profile data kept in sync.
  • An events calendar and ticket sales page for wine dinners, live music, and holiday seatings.
