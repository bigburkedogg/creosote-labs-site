Website and web application development for manufacturers

Many small manufacturers have a website that lists capabilities and a phone number, while quotes still arrive as an emailed drawing with a quantity in the subject line. Product information often lives in a PDF catalog, a price list in Excel, and a set of SolidWorks or AutoCAD files that customers cannot see. Website and web application development applies to the marketing site itself, to how quote requests come in, and to the tools distributors and repeat customers use to check orders and reorder parts.

Creosote Labs builds a marketing site with clear capabilities, materials, tolerances, and certifications, and a quote request form that collects the drawing, material, quantity, and due date in a structured way so the estimator starts with everything needed. For manufacturers with recurring customers, we build a customer portal for order status, reorders, and document downloads such as certificates and drawings. Hosting, domain configuration, and handover documentation are included. Projects are quoted after a scoping call at a fixed price, with no hourly billing.

Common projects

  • A capabilities website listing processes, materials, tolerances, and certifications, with a request-for-quote form on every page.
  • A quote request form that accepts drawing files, material, finish, quantity, and due date and routes the package to the estimator.
  • A customer portal where repeat buyers see open order status, expected ship dates, and past certificates of conformance.
  • An online catalog for standard parts with pricing tiers by quantity and a reorder button for existing accounts.
