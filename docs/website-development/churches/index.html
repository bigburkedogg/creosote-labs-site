Website and web application development for churches

Most churches maintain a website that carries service times, directions, staff, a statement of beliefs, sermon audio or video, and a link to online giving. Many were built on Squarespace, WordPress, or a church-specific builder years ago and are updated by a volunteer. Alongside the site there are usually separate forms for event registration, childcare sign-ups, prayer requests, and small-group sign-ups, plus a YouTube or Facebook feed for the live stream, each managed in its own place.

Creosote Labs builds a church website that a staff member or volunteer can update without a developer: service times and events on the home page, a sermon archive that pulls from the church's existing YouTube or podcast feed, and a giving link to Tithe.ly, Pushpay, or whichever platform the church uses. We also build the forms and tools behind the site, such as event registration with payment, a prayer request queue for the pastoral team, and a members-only directory. Hosting, domain setup, and written handover documentation are included.

Common projects
