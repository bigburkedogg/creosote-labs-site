Website and web application development for chiropractic offices

Chiropractic office websites usually need to do 3 things: explain the conditions the doctor treats, make it easy to book a first appointment, and get new-patient paperwork completed before the visit. Many offices have a site built years ago on WordPress or Wix, with online scheduling through a widget from Jane App or ChiroTouch and intake forms that are still printed PDFs. Website and web application development covers the site itself and the tools patients use through it.

We build a marketing site with service pages, insurance and pricing information, and a booking path that connects to the scheduling system the office already runs. Beyond the site, we build the patient-facing tools that replace paper: an online intake form that writes to the patient record, a records request form for attorneys and other providers, and a portal where patients can see their care plan and remaining visits. Hosting, domain setup, and documentation are part of every project.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with pages for each condition and technique, accepted insurance plans, and new-patient pricing, with a book-now button on every page.
  • An online new-patient intake form covering history, consent, and insurance card upload, delivered to the front desk before the first visit.
  • A booking page that shows the doctor's real availability and confirms the appointment by text.
  • A patient portal that displays the current care plan, visits completed, and prepaid visits remaining.
  • A records request form for attorneys and referring physicians that logs each request and its due date.

Frequently asked questions

  • Can the site connect to the scheduler the office already uses?

    Most chiropractic scheduling systems offer an embeddable booking widget or an API. We use what the office's system provides and confirm the integration during the scoping call.

  • What does the office need to provide for a website project?

    Service descriptions, photos of the office and staff, the accepted insurance list, and the current intake paperwork. We handle the writing, structure, hosting, and domain setup.
