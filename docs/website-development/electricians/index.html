Website and web application development for electrical contractors

Many electrical contractors get work from referrals, a Google Business Profile, and a website that has not changed since the company was founded. The site usually lists services and a phone number, while quote requests come in by phone and get written on a notepad. Website and web application development applies to the marketing site itself and to what sits behind it: a service request form, an online estimate for common jobs, and a customer page for project updates.

Creosote Labs builds a site that states what the company does, the areas it serves, its license number, and how to book a visit, with a request form that lands in the office inbox or directly in the dispatch software. For companies that want more, we add a quoting tool for standard jobs such as EV charger installs or panel replacements and a portal where commercial clients can see work orders and invoices. Hosting, domain setup, and documentation are part of the handover.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with service pages for residential, commercial, and EV charger work, license and insurance details, and a booking form.
  • A service request form that collects the address, photos of the panel, and a description of the problem, then creates the job in Jobber.
  • An instant estimate page for fixed-scope jobs such as ceiling fan installs and outlet additions, priced from the company's rate sheet.
  • A commercial client portal showing open work orders, completed inspections, and outstanding invoices for each property.
  • A maintenance reminder page that lets past customers schedule an annual panel and surge protection check.
