Website and web application development for general contractors

A general contractor's website usually does 2 jobs: it shows past projects and services to homeowners and commercial clients checking references, and it takes in new leads. Many contractors have a site built years ago on WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace, with a contact form that sends an email and a project gallery that has not been updated. Behind the site, bid requests, selections, and progress updates are often handled by phone, text, and email.

Creosote Labs builds marketing sites that state the services offered, the project types, the service area, and how to request a bid, and builds the customer-facing tools behind them: a quote request form that collects the details an estimator needs, a client portal where owners approve selections and change orders, and a photo-based progress page per project. We set up hosting and the domain, connect the site to the company's existing CRM or email, and hand it over with documentation.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with project galleries by type, a service area map, and a bid request form.
  • A quote request form that asks for project type, square footage, timeline, and photos, and routes the request to the right estimator.
  • A client portal where homeowners review selections, approve change orders, and see the schedule.
  • A subcontractor prequalification form that collects license, insurance, and trade information.
  • A progress page for each project where the client sees the latest photos and notes from the field.

Frequently asked questions

  • What does a general contractor website cost?

  • What does the company need to provide?

    Project photos, a list of services and project types, license and insurance details to display, and access to the current domain registrar and any existing site. We write the copy from a conversation with the owner.
