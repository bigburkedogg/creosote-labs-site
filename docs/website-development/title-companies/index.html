Website and web application development for title and escrow companies

A title company's website is usually where agents, lenders, and the public look for the rate calculator, the office locations, the order form, and wire fraud warnings. Many of these sites were built years ago on WordPress or a template from the underwriter and have not been updated since. Order requests often come in as PDF forms attached to emails, buyers call the office to find out whether their file has recorded, and net sheet requests are handled by hand.

Creosote Labs builds the marketing site and the tools behind it. A typical project is a site with services, office locations, staff directory, and a clear place to open an order, plus an online order form that reaches the right escrow officer, a rate and fee calculator agents can use to prepare net sheets, and a secure status page where buyers and sellers can see where their file stands. We handle hosting and domain setup and hand over documentation.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with offices, staff, services, and a wire fraud notice shown before any wiring instructions are discussed.
  • An online order form for agents and lenders that collects the contract, parties, and property details and routes the order to the right office.
  • A rate and fee calculator that produces a buyer or seller net sheet an agent can download and share.
  • A file status page where parties log in to see signing, funding, and recording progress without calling the office.
  • A document upload portal for buyers and sellers to return signed disclosures and identification securely.
  • A page for each escrow officer with a direct order link that agents can bookmark.

Frequently asked questions

  • How is a website project priced?

    Website and application projects are quoted after a scoping call. The price is fixed for the agreed scope, with no hourly billing.

  • Can the order form feed our production platform?

    That depends on the platform. Where it offers an integration or import, the form can create the order directly; otherwise the form delivers the order to the assigned officer in a format that is quick to enter. We confirm the approach during scoping.

  • What do we need to provide?

    Access to the current domain registrar and hosting, office and staff details, the current fee schedule and rate tables, and any branding or disclosure requirements from the underwriter.
