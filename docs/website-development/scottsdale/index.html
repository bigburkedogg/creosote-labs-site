Website and web application development in Scottsdale

Many Scottsdale businesses are resorts, spas, golf and tour businesses, medical and dental practices, real estate offices, and financial and professional firms, and they often compete for customers who find them online first. Websites for these businesses often sit on WordPress, Squarespace, or Wix, with bookings handled through a separate tool such as Mindbody, FareHarbor, or a practice scheduling system. Customers expect to see services, pricing, and a way to book or request a quote without calling.

Creosote Labs builds websites and web applications for Scottsdale businesses. A project can be a marketing site with clear services, pricing, and a contact or booking path, a customer-facing tool such as a quote request form, intake form, or client portal, or a web application that replaces a manual process for staff and customers. Projects are quoted after a scoping call at a fixed price, and each one includes hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.
