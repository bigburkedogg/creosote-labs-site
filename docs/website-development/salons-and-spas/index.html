Website and web application development for salons and spas

Salons and spas often rely on a booking platform's built-in page, a social media profile, or a site built years ago that no longer matches the current service menu. Clients look for services, prices, stylist bios, hours, and a way to book before they call. Website work for this kind of business covers the marketing site, the booking and gift card flow, and the forms a spa collects before a first appointment, such as consultation and consent forms.

Creosote Labs builds marketing sites with a complete service menu, clear pricing, stylist and technician pages, and booking that connects to the platform the business already uses, whether that is Vagaro, Booksy, Square Appointments, GlossGenius, or Mindbody. We also build online intake and consent forms, gift card and membership pages, and staff-facing tools when a business needs them. Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation are included in every project.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with the full service menu, prices, and a booking button that opens the salon's existing scheduler.
  • Stylist and esthetician pages with specialties, portfolio photos, and a direct booking link for each person.
  • An online consultation form for color, lash, or skincare services that reaches the technician before the appointment.
  • A digital consent and intake form for spa treatments that stores signed copies with the client record.
  • A gift card and membership page where clients purchase and check balances online.
  • A careers page with an application form for stylists and booth renters.
