Website and web application development for plumbing companies

A plumbing company's website usually has one job: turn a search for an emergency plumber or a water heater replacement into a phone call or a booked appointment. Many plumbing websites are built on WordPress or a template from the field service vendor, list services without prices, and route every request through a contact form that reaches the office hours later. Online booking through ServiceTitan or Jobber exists but is often not connected to the site in a way customers find.

Creosote Labs builds the marketing site and the tools behind it. That means service pages with clear descriptions and starting prices where the company wants to publish them, a booking flow connected to the dispatch platform, a quote request form that collects photos and the fixture in question, and service area pages for each town the trucks cover. Projects are quoted after a scoping call at a fixed price, and handover includes hosting, domain setup, and written documentation.

What we do

  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with a service page for each line of work, from drain cleaning to repiping, and a click-to-call header on mobile.
  • An online booking page that writes appointments straight into Housecall Pro with the customer's address and problem description.
  • A quote request form for water heater and fixture replacements that asks for a photo and the model number before the office calls back.

    Yes, when the platform supports it. ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber each offer online booking that can be placed on the site, and we can build a custom booking flow when the built-in one does not fit.
