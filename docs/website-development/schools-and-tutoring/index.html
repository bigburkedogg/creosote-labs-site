Website and web application development for schools and tutoring businesses

The website for a school or tutoring business is where families compare programs, check tuition and hours, and decide whether to reach out. Many are built on Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress, with a Google Form or a link to Calendly for inquiries and a PDF for the enrollment packet. Tutoring centers often want families to book an assessment or a trial session directly, and schools often need an application process that does not depend on paper forms and email attachments.

Creosote Labs builds marketing sites with clear program descriptions, tuition, and a way to book or apply, along with the customer-facing tools behind them. That can be an online enrollment application, a trial session booking form that lands on the tutor's calendar, or a parent portal where families see schedules, invoices, and progress reports. Projects include hosting and domain setup, and we hand over the site with documentation so staff can update it.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site for a tutoring center with program pages, pricing, tutor profiles, and a trial session booking form.
  • An online enrollment application for a private school that collects family details, documents, and a deposit in one flow.
  • A parent portal where families view upcoming sessions, download invoices, and read term progress reports.
  • A summer program registration page with session capacity limits, waitlists, and automatic confirmation emails.
  • A tutor-facing web application for logging sessions and notes from a phone between appointments.
