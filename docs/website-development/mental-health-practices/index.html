Website and web application development for mental health practices

Most mental health practices are found through a Psychology Today profile, an insurance directory, or a referral, and the practice website is where a prospective client goes next to check clinician bios, fees, insurance accepted, and how to get started. Many practice sites are a template with a contact form, while intake paperwork, consent forms, and the good-faith estimate are still emailed as PDFs or handled through a client portal that is separate from the site. Website work covers both the marketing site and those client-facing steps.

Creosote Labs builds practice websites with clinician pages, a plain list of services and fees, accepted insurance, and a request-appointment form that routes to the right person. Where the practice wants more, we build the client-facing tools around it: a screening form that asks the questions the front desk asks on the first call, a page that explains what happens after a request, and links that hand off to the SimplePractice or TherapyNotes client portal for scheduling and paperwork. Hosting, the domain, and documentation are part of the handover.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A practice website with a page per clinician listing specialties, license type, session format, and current availability.
  • A new-client request form that collects concern, insurance, and preferred times and sends a confirmation with next steps and the practice's fee notice.
  • A services and fees page with a good-faith estimate calculator based on session type and frequency.
  • A group practice site that lets the office manager add or remove clinicians and update availability without editing code.
  • A referral form for physicians and schools that submits directly to the intake coordinator's queue.

Frequently asked questions

  • Can the site handle intake paperwork and scheduling?

    It can link to or embed the client portal the practice already uses, such as SimplePractice or TherapyNotes, for signed consents and scheduling. We can also build a screening form on the site itself; anything that collects health information is discussed during scoping so hosting and agreements are set up appropriately.

  • How much does a practice website cost?

    Website and application projects are quoted after a scoping call, at a fixed price with no hourly billing. The quote depends on the number of pages, forms, and integrations.

  • What does the practice need to provide?

    Clinician bios and photos, the fee schedule and insurance list, and access to the domain registrar. We handle hosting setup, domain configuration, and documentation at handover.
