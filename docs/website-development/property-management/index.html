Website and web application development for property management companies

A property management company's website usually has two audiences: prospective tenants looking for available units and an application, and property owners deciding whether to hand over a rental. Many companies run on an AppFolio or Buildium tenant portal for payments and maintenance requests, with a separate marketing site that lists vacancies by hand and links out to the portal. Owner inquiries often arrive through a generic contact form, while rental applications, showing requests, and maintenance forms live in different places.

Creosote Labs builds the marketing site and the tools around it: a listings page that pulls available units from the management platform, an owner inquiry form that collects the property details a manager needs before the first call, and a showing request or maintenance form that lands where staff will see it. Hosting, domain configuration, and documentation are part of the handover. Website and application projects are quoted after a scoping call, at a fixed price with no hourly billing.

What we do

  • Marketing websites with clear services, pricing, and a way to book or contact the business.
  • Customer-facing tools such as quote requests, bookings, intake forms, and portals.
  • Web applications that replace manual processes for staff and customers.
  • Hosting setup, domain configuration, and handover with documentation.

Common projects

  • A marketing site with services, management fees, and a page for each property type the company handles.
  • An available-units page that syncs vacancies, photos, and rents from AppFolio or Buildium instead of being updated by hand.
  • An owner inquiry form that asks for address, unit count, current rent, and condition, and produces a management proposal draft.
  • A self-scheduling tool for showings that checks the leasing agent's calendar and confirms by text.
  • A maintenance request form for properties not on the tenant portal that creates a work order and notifies the assigned vendor.
  • An owner portal for companies whose platform does not include one, with statements, invoices, and inspection reports by property.

Frequently asked questions

  • We already have a tenant portal through our software. Do we still need a website?

    The portal handles payments and requests for existing tenants. A website covers the rest: vacancies, owner acquisition, and the forms that currently go through email or a generic contact page. The two link to each other.

  • Can the site show our vacancies automatically?

    Yes, where the management platform provides a listing feed or API. The scoping call confirms what the platform makes available and the quote reflects it.
