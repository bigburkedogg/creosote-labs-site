Team AI training in Sedona

Front desk, reservations, sales, marketing, and office staff at Sedona's hotels, tour companies, galleries, real estate offices, and professional practices often spend much of the day writing: guest emails, itineraries, listing copy, social posts, proposals, and internal notes. Many of these teams already have an AI assistant available through Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or a Claude account, and often it sits unused because nobody has shown staff how it applies to their specific work.

Creosote Labs delivers team AI training on site in Sedona or remotely. The workshop is one day, in the AI tool the company already uses, with each participant working on their own computer and their own documents. Everyone produces real work during the session and documents their own process, and the team receives a written guide plus a 30-day follow-up session. The price is $1,000 per person for up to 10 people with a 5-person minimum, travel included; a program with 3 monthly follow-up sessions is $1,400 per person.
