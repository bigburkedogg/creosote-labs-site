Team AI training for title and escrow companies

Escrow officers, assistants, and processors spend their day in the production platform and in email, moving between title commitments, closing instructions, purchase contracts, and the parties waiting on each of them. Many offices already have Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, with Copilot or Gemini included in or available for the subscription, and some staff have tried it on their own. Use across the office tends to be uneven, with no shared way of working and no written guidance on what the tool should and should not touch.

The workshop is one day, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses. Each participant works at their own computer on their own files: summarizing a lengthy commitment with its exceptions, pulling the conditions out of a lender's closing instructions, drafting a plain-language explanation of a settlement statement for a first-time buyer, or writing the status email for a file that is waiting on a payoff. Everyone leaves with a working setup, a written guide for the office, and a 30-day follow-up session.

What we do

  • One day, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses.
  • Participants work on their own computers with their own documents.
  • Each participant produces real work during the session and documents their own process.
  • A written guide for the team and a 30-day follow-up session are included.

Common projects

  • Each escrow officer builds a prompt that summarizes a title commitment and lists the Schedule B exceptions that need curative work.
  • Processors set up a reusable template that drafts the opening letter to buyer and seller from the purchase contract.
  • The front desk practices answering common buyer questions about earnest money and closing costs against an approved plain-language reference.
  • The office writes a shared policy on what may be entered into the AI tool and what stays inside the production platform.
  • A follow-up session 30 days later reviews what each person kept using and where the guide needs updating.
