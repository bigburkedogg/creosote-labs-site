Team AI training for wholesale distributors

Inside sales, purchasing, and office staff at a wholesale distributor spend much of the day in email, in the accounting system, and in Excel: answering price and availability questions, keying orders from emailed purchase orders, chasing vendors on late shipments, and preparing customer statements. Many of these companies already have Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and with it access to Copilot or Gemini, but the tools are often not set up on the company's own catalog, price lists, and correspondence.

Creosote Labs runs a single-day workshop for distributor staff, on site or remote, using the AI tool already in place. Each participant works on their own computer with their own open orders, vendor emails, and price sheets, and produces real work during the session: a drafted order confirmation, a summarized vendor thread, a reformatted price list. Everyone documents their own process, and the team leaves with a written guide. A 30-day follow-up session is included. Pricing is $1,000 per person for up to 10 people, with a 5-person minimum.

Common projects

  • Inside sales staff drafting order confirmations and backorder notices from the company's own templates.
  • Purchasing staff summarizing long vendor email threads into a status line for each open purchase order.
  • Office staff turning a vendor's PDF price update into the company's price list format in Excel.
  • Sales reps preparing a one-page account summary from order history before a customer visit.
  • A shared library of the team's recurring prompts, kept in the written guide.
