Team AI training in Scottsdale

Teams at Scottsdale financial firms, insurance agencies, medical and dental practices, real estate offices, and hospitality companies often already have an AI tool available through Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or a ChatGPT or Claude subscription. Usage often stops at occasional email drafting when staff have not been shown how to apply the tool to the specific documents they produce every week: client reviews, policy summaries, treatment plans, listing descriptions, group proposals, and internal reports.

Creosote Labs delivers team AI training in Scottsdale as a single day, on site or remote, in the tool the company already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own files, so a claims processor practices on real claims and an office manager practices on the actual patient recall letters. Each person produces finished work during the session and writes down their own process. The day includes a written guide for the team and a 30-day follow-up session.
