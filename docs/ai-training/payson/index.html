Team AI training in Payson

Offices in Payson, whether a medical practice, a contractor, a school, or a real estate office, generally already have an AI tool included in the software they pay for. Microsoft 365 includes Copilot, Google Workspace includes Gemini, and Claude is available on its own. Those tools often go unused because nobody on staff has been shown how to apply them to the letters, estimates, reports, and spreadsheets the office produces every week. Team AI training addresses that gap directly.

Creosote Labs delivers the workshop in Payson, on site at the business or remotely, in the AI tool the team already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own documents, produce real work during the session, and write down their own process as they go. The team receives a written guide, and a 30-day follow-up session is included. Pricing is $1,000 per person for up to 10 people, with a 5-person minimum and travel included; a program with 3 monthly follow-up sessions is $1,400 per person.
