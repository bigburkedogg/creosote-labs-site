Team AI training in Kingman

Offices in Kingman generally already have an AI tool available through the software they pay for: Copilot inside Microsoft 365, Gemini inside Google Workspace, or Claude on its own. Medical and dental front desks, trucking and logistics offices, insurance agencies, real estate brokerages, and city and county departments produce a steady volume of letters, summaries, reports, and customer replies. Training applies where a team has the tool but has not worked out how to use it on those documents.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop at the team's office in Kingman or remotely, in the AI tool the company already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own documents, produce real work during the session, and document their own process. Each person leaves with a working setup and a written guide, and a 30-day follow-up session is included. The workshop is $1,000 per person for up to 10 people with a 5-person minimum, travel included; a program with 3 monthly follow-ups is $1,400 per person.
