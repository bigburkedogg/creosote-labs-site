Team AI training for startups and founders

Startup teams tend to adopt AI tools one person at a time. One engineer uses Claude for code review, a founder drafts investor emails in a different tool, and the rest of the team has a subscription with no shared setup. The work those tools could handle, such as product specs, customer emails, meeting summaries, and hiring materials, gets done differently by every person. Team AI training applies where a company wants one shared, documented way of working across those tasks.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own documents: the current product spec, the live support queue, the next board deck. Each person produces real work during the session and documents their own process, and the team leaves with a working setup and a written guide. A 30-day follow-up session is included.
