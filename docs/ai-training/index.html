Services

Team AI training

A one-day, hands-on workshop delivered on a team's own tools, documents, and templates. Every participant leaves with a working setup and a written guide.

What we do

  • One day, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses.
  • Participants work on their own computers with their own documents.
  • Each participant produces real work during the session and documents their own process.
  • A written guide for the team and a 30-day follow-up session are included.

Pricing

$1,000 per person for up to 10 people, with a 5-person minimum. Larger teams are quoted. Travel is included. A program with three monthly follow-up sessions is $1,400 per person.
