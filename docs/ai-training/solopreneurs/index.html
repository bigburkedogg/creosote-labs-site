Team AI training for solopreneurs and independent professionals

Independent professionals rarely have anyone to learn a new tool from. Many already pay for ChatGPT, Claude, or Google Gemini, use it for the occasional email, and have not connected it to the documents they produce every week: proposals, session notes, client updates, invoices, and marketing copy. The tools sit alongside Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, Canva, and Mailchimp without a defined place in the workflow. Training applies wherever the owner writes the same kind of document repeatedly and wants a reliable process for producing it.

Creosote Labs runs the workshop in one day, on site or remote, in the AI tool each participant already uses. The session has a 5-person minimum, so one-person businesses can fill it with the people they work alongside: a bookkeeper, a virtual assistant, subcontractors, or other independent professionals. Each participant works on their own computer with their own files and leaves with a working setup and a written record of their process. A written guide and a 30-day follow-up session are included. The fee is $1,000 per person.
