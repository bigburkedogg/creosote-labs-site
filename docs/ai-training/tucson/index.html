Team AI training in Tucson

Many teams in Tucson already have an AI tool available through a subscription the company pays for: Copilot inside Microsoft 365, Gemini inside Google Workspace, or ChatGPT or Claude accounts staff signed up for on their own. Offices at medical and dental practices, law and accounting firms, contractors, and nonprofits often have a few people who use these tools regularly and many who have not tried them on real work. Team AI training applies when a business wants the whole team working the same way, on its own documents.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop for a Tucson team, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own documents, produce real work during the session, and document their own process. The team receives a written guide and a 30-day follow-up session. Training is $1,000 per person for up to 10 people, with a 5-person minimum, and travel to Tucson is included.
