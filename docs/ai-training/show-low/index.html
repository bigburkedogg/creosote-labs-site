Team AI training in Show Low

Show Low's employers include medical and dental offices, contractors, real estate and property management companies, banks and insurance agencies, retailers, and the hospitality businesses that support tourism in the White Mountains. Teams at these businesses often already have Copilot through Microsoft 365 or Gemini through Google Workspace, and staff use them unevenly or not at all. Team AI training applies where an office wants everyone working the same way, on the documents and messages they produce every day.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop on site in Show Low or remotely, in the AI tool the company already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own files, so a dental office practices on treatment plans and recall letters while a contractor's office practices on estimates and change orders. Each participant produces real work during the day and writes down their own process. A written guide and a 30-day follow-up session are included.
