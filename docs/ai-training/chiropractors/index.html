Team AI training for chiropractic offices

Staff at a chiropractic office often spend much of the day producing written material: new-patient welcome emails, care plan explanations, insurance appeal letters, personal injury narratives, records request responses, and social posts for the practice. Most of that work is done in Word, Google Docs, Gmail, or Outlook alongside ChiroTouch or Jane App. Team AI training teaches the front desk, billing staff, and doctors to do that writing faster with the AI tool the office already has.

The workshop is 1 day, on site or remote, in Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude. Each participant works on their own computer with the practice's actual templates: the billing coordinator drafts an appeal from a real denial, the front desk writes reminder and reactivation messages, and the doctor turns exam notes into a patient-friendly report. Everyone documents their own process, the team receives a written guide, and a 30-day follow-up session is included.

Common projects

  • An insurance appeal letter template the billing coordinator can regenerate from any denial code and the patient's visit history.
  • A set of reusable prompts that produce welcome, reminder, and missed-appointment messages in the office's tone.
  • A process for converting the doctor's exam and re-exam notes into a plain-language care plan summary for the patient.
  • A records request workflow that drafts the cover letter and itemizes the documents being sent.
  • A monthly newsletter and social post routine built from the practice's own content calendar.
