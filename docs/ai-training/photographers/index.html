Team AI training for photography studios

Photography studios with a few staff or associate shooters typically share a set of written materials: inquiry reply templates, a pricing guide, session prep and what-to-wear emails, gallery delivery messages, blog posts, and social captions. This writing is repeated for every client and usually done in Gmail, HoneyBook, or Dubsado. Studios often have access to an AI tool already, through Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or a Claude account, but have not put their own documents into it.

Creosote Labs runs a one-day workshop, on site or remote, in the AI tool the studio already uses. Each participant works on their own computer with their own templates and client emails, producing real work during the session: an inquiry reply, a session prep email, a blog post from a recent shoot, a caption set. Each person documents their own process, and the team leaves with a written guide and a 30-day follow-up session. Training is $1,000 per person for up to 10 people, with a 5-person minimum.
