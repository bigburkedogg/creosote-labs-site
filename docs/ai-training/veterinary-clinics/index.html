Team AI training for veterinary clinics

The people in a veterinary clinic who write the most are often not the veterinarians. Technicians write discharge summaries and callback notes, the front desk answers email about appointment prep and billing, and the practice manager writes the job postings, the client-facing policies, and the monthly staff update. Most of that writing happens in Outlook, Gmail, or Google Docs alongside the practice management system, which is where an AI tool such as Copilot, Gemini, or Claude can be put to work.

Team AI training from Creosote Labs is a one-day workshop held at the clinic or remotely, in the AI tool the clinic already uses. Each participant works on their own computer with their own documents: a technician drafts tomorrow's discharge instructions, the front desk builds reply templates for the questions that come in every day, and the practice manager writes the next protocol update. Everyone leaves with a working setup, a written guide for the team, and a 30-day follow-up session.

What we do

  • One day, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses.

  • Each participant produces real work during the session and documents their own process.
  • A written guide for the team and a 30-day follow-up session are included.

Common projects

  • A front-desk reply library for the most common client emails, from pre-surgery fasting instructions to payment questions, each checked against clinic policy.
  • A discharge summary workflow where the technician pastes the doctor's notes and receives a draft the owner can follow at home.
  • A protocol document rewritten from years of accumulated memos into a single current version the whole staff can search.
  • A job posting and interview question set for a technician opening, built from the clinic's own description of the role.
  • A client education handout set for dental cleanings, weight management, and senior pet care, written in the clinic's voice.
