Team AI training in Page

Teams in Page tend to be small and seasonal: a front desk, a reservations office, a few guides or drivers, a kitchen, and an owner who handles the books in QuickBooks. Many already have Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, which means Copilot or Gemini may be available without a new subscription, and some have tried ChatGPT or Claude on their own. Team AI training applies to any group that writes guest emails, confirmations, schedules, and reports as part of the job.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop in Page, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own documents, so a reservations agent leaves with a working setup for guest replies and a guide leaves with one for trip briefings. Each person documents their own process during the session. The team receives a written guide, and a 30-day follow-up session is included.
