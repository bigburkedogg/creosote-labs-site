Team AI training for physical therapy clinics

Staff at a physical therapy clinic do a good deal of writing between patients. Therapists complete daily notes, re-evaluations, and progress reports in the EMR; the front desk answers insurance questions, chases authorizations, and sends appointment reminders; the owner or office manager prepares payer appeals, job postings, and outreach letters to referring physicians. Many clinics already have Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and with it access to Copilot or Gemini, and training on those tools is usually left to each person to figure out.

Team AI training from Creosote Labs is a one-day workshop, on site or remote, in the AI tool the clinic already pays for. Each participant works on their own computer with their own material: a therapist drafts a progress report from a de-identified evaluation, the front desk builds reusable replies to common patient questions, and the office manager sets up a template for authorization appeals. Everyone leaves with a working setup, their own documented process, and a written team guide. A 30-day follow-up session is included.

Common projects

  • A session exercise in which therapists turn a de-identified daily note into a physician progress report using the clinic's own report format.
  • A front desk exercise building a library of replies for insurance, copay, and scheduling questions that match the clinic's policies.
  • An office manager exercise drafting an appeal letter for a denied authorization from the payer's denial and the plan of care.
  • A group exercise summarizing a month of patient satisfaction survey responses into themes for the next staff meeting.
  • A follow-up session 30 days later that reviews what each participant has kept using and refines their written process.
