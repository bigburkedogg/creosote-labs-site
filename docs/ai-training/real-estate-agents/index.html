Team AI training for real estate agents

A real estate agent's week involves a great deal of writing: listing descriptions, buyer and seller emails, offer summaries, market updates, social media posts, and newsletters, along with reading contracts, inspection reports, and HOA documents. Many agents already have access to an AI tool through Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or a brokerage subscription and use it occasionally. Team AI training is for agents and their teams who want to use it on their actual work every day.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop on site or remote, in the AI tool the team already uses. Each participant works on their own computer with their own listings, templates, and client correspondence, produces real work during the session, and documents their own process. The team leaves with a written guide covering the prompts and workflows built that day, and a 30-day follow-up session is included.

What we do

  • One day, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses.
  • Participants work on their own computers with their own documents.
  • Each participant produces real work during the session and documents their own process.
  • A written guide for the team and a 30-day follow-up session are included.

Common projects

  • A listing description workflow that takes the agent's notes and photos and produces MLS-ready copy in the agent's voice.
  • An inspection report summary that lists the findings a buyer should discuss and drafts the repair request.
  • A market update template that turns monthly MLS statistics into a newsletter for the agent's sphere.
  • A set of saved prompts for buyer consultations, showing feedback, and offer cover letters.
  • A process for reviewing HOA documents and CC&Rs and producing a plain summary for the client.
