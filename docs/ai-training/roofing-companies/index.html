Team AI training for roofing companies

The office and sales staff at a roofing company spend much of the day writing: proposals for homeowners, supplement requests to insurance adjusters, scope explanations, material orders, crew instructions, and answers to the same questions about warranties and financing. Many of them already have Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude available through a company account and use it occasionally, without a shared way of working. Training applies to exactly this kind of repeated writing and to reading long documents such as adjuster estimates and manufacturer specifications.

Creosote Labs runs a one-day, hands-on workshop for the team, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses. Participants bring their own laptops and their own files: last month's proposals, a denied supplement, the warranty FAQ, the crew checklist. Each person builds a working setup for their role during the day, such as a sales rep's proposal drafting process or an office manager's supplement workflow, and documents it. The team leaves with a written guide, and a 30-day follow-up session is included.

Common projects

  • A sales rep's setup that drafts a homeowner proposal cover letter from the estimate and inspection notes.
  • An office manager's process for turning an adjuster's scope and the crew's photos into a supplement request.
  • A production coordinator's crew brief, generated from the signed contract and material order each morning.
  • A shared set of prompts for answering warranty, financing, and scheduling questions in the company's voice.
  • A reading workflow that summarizes a manufacturer's installation spec or a commercial bid package before a meeting.
