Team AI training in Phoenix

Many Phoenix businesses have already turned on AI features in the software they pay for. Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, and Claude are available to office staff at insurance agencies, law firms, medical practices, and construction companies across the metro. What is often missing is a working method: which tool to open for a renewal letter, how to give it the firm's template, and how to check the result before it goes to a client. Without that, usage often stays with a few people experimenting on their own.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop for teams in Phoenix, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses. Participants work on their own computers with their own documents, so a paralegal drafts a real engagement letter during the session. Each person documents their own process and leaves with a working setup. A written guide for the team and a 30-day follow-up session are included. Pricing is $1,000 per person for up to 10 people, with a 5-person minimum, and travel is included.
