Team AI training for event and wedding planners

Planning teams spend much of the week producing documents: client proposals, vendor inquiry emails, contracts, day-of timelines, welcome letters, and post-event recaps. Most of that writing starts from a previous version in Google Docs or Word, or from a template in Aisle Planner or HoneyBook. Coordinators and assistants often have access to Google Gemini or Microsoft 365 Copilot through the company's accounts without a settled way of using them on this work.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop, on site or remote, in the AI tool the business already uses. Each participant works on their own computer with their own proposals, timelines, and vendor correspondence, produces real work during the session, and documents their own process. The team leaves with a written guide and a working setup, and a 30-day follow-up session is included.

Common projects

  • A lead coordinator drafts a full-service proposal from an inquiry form using the company's pricing and package descriptions.
  • An assistant produces a set of vendor outreach emails for a single event from the vendor list and event details.
  • A planner builds a day-of timeline and vendor call sheet from a ceremony time and a list of confirmed vendors.
  • A team member turns post-event notes into a client recap and an internal lessons file.
  • The team writes a shared prompt guide for the documents it produces most often.
