Team AI training for consultants and coaches

Consulting and coaching teams produce a large amount of written material for each client: discovery summaries, proposals, workshop agendas, slide decks, session notes, and reports. Most of it is written in Google Docs, Microsoft Word, or PowerPoint from a previous version. Practitioners who have tried ChatGPT or Gemini on their own often find that a general prompt does not know the practice's frameworks, tone, or document formats.

The Creosote Labs workshop runs for one day, on site or remote, in the AI tool the practice already uses. Each participant brings their own current work: a proposal in progress, last week's session notes, a report due to a client. They finish real deliverables during the session and write down the process they used. The team leaves with a written guide, and a 30-day follow-up session is included.

What we do

  • One day, on site or remote, in the AI tool the company already uses.
  • Participants work on their own computers with their own documents.
  • Each participant produces real work during the session and documents their own process.
  • A written guide for the team and a 30-day follow-up session are included.

Common projects

  • A participant drafts a proposal for a live prospect from their discovery notes and the practice's proposal template.
  • A participant turns a recorded session into client-ready notes and a follow-up email in their own voice.
  • A participant builds a reusable prompt that produces a workshop agenda from a client's stated goals.
  • A participant converts a written framework into a slide deck outline in the practice's standard format.
  • A participant sets up a reviewer that checks a client report against the original statement of work.
