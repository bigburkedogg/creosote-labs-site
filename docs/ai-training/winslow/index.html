Team AI training in Winslow

Many businesses in Winslow already pay for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and the AI tools in those subscriptions, Copilot and Gemini, often go unused. Team AI training applies wherever a group of people write, summarize, or organize information as part of their job.

Creosote Labs delivers a one-day, hands-on workshop in Winslow, on site or remote, using the AI tool the company already has.
