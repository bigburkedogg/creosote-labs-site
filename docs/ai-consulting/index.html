Services

AI consulting

We assess where AI tools fit in a business, implement them, and train staff to use them in their day-to-day work.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Pricing

Engagements begin with a one-day operations review at $3,000, which produces a written assessment and a quote for each recommendation. Implementation is quoted from the review. Team training is $1,000 per person.
