AI consulting in Scottsdale

Many Scottsdale businesses are in tourism, healthcare, financial and insurance services, real estate, and professional services. Businesses in those industries typically work in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, with a line-of-business system underneath such as HubSpot or Salesforce for sales, QuickBooks for accounting, or a practice management or property management platform. AI tools such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, and Claude fit best where staff spend hours each week writing, summarizing, and preparing documents from those systems.

Creosote Labs serves businesses in Scottsdale and the Phoenix metro.
