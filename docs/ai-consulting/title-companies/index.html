AI consulting for title and escrow companies

Many title and escrow companies run on a production platform such as SoftPro, Qualia, ResWare, or RamQuest, with Outlook for communication with lenders, agents, and buyers, and Excel for anything the platform does not track. The work is document-heavy: title commitments, curative letters, settlement statements, closing disclosures, wire instructions, and post-closing packets. Much of the day goes to reading incoming documents, rekeying details into the platform, and writing status updates to the parties on each file.

Creosote Labs assesses where AI tools fit in a title operation and sets them up on the company's own files. That usually means configuring Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude with the company's templates, checklists, and underwriting guidelines, then building the specific uses: summarizing a recorded deed or a lender's closing instructions, drafting a status email from the file notes, or checking a settlement statement against the purchase contract. Staff are trained on their own files, with a written guide for the office.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An assistant configured with the company's underwriting guidelines that flags exceptions in a preliminary title report and drafts the curative request.
  • A setup that reads lender closing instructions and produces a list of conditions for the escrow officer to confirm before funding.
  • Status update emails to agents, lenders, and buyers drafted from the file notes in the production platform and reviewed before they go out.
  • A comparison of the settlement statement against the purchase contract and the lender's fee sheet that lists discrepancies for review.
  • A written guide for the office covering which tasks the AI tool handles and which stay with the escrow officer.
