AI consulting for startups and founders

Many startups run on a small set of tools adopted in the first year: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for email and documents, Slack for internal communication, Notion for wikis and planning, HubSpot for the sales pipeline, Stripe for billing, and QuickBooks Online for the books. The founders write most of the investor updates, customer proposals, onboarding documents, and job descriptions themselves. AI consulting applies to the recurring writing, research, and reporting that sits across those tools.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of how the company actually works, then delivers a written assessment of where AI tools save time and a quote for each recommendation. Implementation typically means setting up Claude, Gemini, or Copilot with the company's own templates, product documentation, and past customer correspondence, so drafts come out in the company's voice. We then train the team on their own work and leave a written guide.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An investor update workflow that pulls the month's metrics from Stripe and HubSpot into a draft memo in the company's existing format.
  • A support inbox setup in which Claude drafts replies from the product documentation and a person reviews and sends them.
  • A sales proposal assistant built on the company's past proposals and current pricing sheet.
  • A hiring workflow that produces the job description, screening questions, and candidate summaries from one role brief.
  • A research process that turns competitor and market notes into a standard one-page brief for the founders.
