AI consulting for financial advisory practices

Financial advisory practices typically run on a CRM such as Redtail, Wealthbox, or Salesforce, a planning platform such as eMoney, MoneyGuidePro, or RightCapital, a custodian portal such as Schwab Advisor Center or Fidelity Wealthscape, and Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace for email and documents. Client meetings produce notes, follow-up letters, financial plans, and account paperwork, and much of that writing is done by hand. AI consulting applies where that drafting and summarizing takes staff time every week.

Creosote Labs begins with a one-day operations review of the practice, then sets up the AI tool the firm already licenses, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot or Claude, with the firm's own meeting note template, client letter formats, and plan summary language. We automate the first draft of meeting summaries, follow-up emails, and annual review letters, keep every output inside the firm's existing review and archiving process, and train the advisors and client service staff on their own client work.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A meeting summary workflow that turns an advisor's dictated notes into a CRM note and a client follow-up email in the firm's format.
  • An annual review letter draft produced from the client's plan summary and the prior year's letter.
  • A first-draft generator for client-facing explanations of a plan recommendation, written at the reading level the firm specifies.
  • A prospect inquiry triage that reads incoming emails and drafts a reply and a Redtail task for the advisor.
  • A written AI use guide for the practice covering what staff may and may not put into the tool.
