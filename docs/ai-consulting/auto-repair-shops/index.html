AI consulting for auto repair shops

Many independent auto repair shops run on a shop management system such as Tekmetric, Shop-Ware, Shopmonkey, or Mitchell 1 Manager SE, with QuickBooks for accounting and a parts ordering tool such as PartsTech. In a typical shop, the service advisor writes the estimate, the technician fills out a digital vehicle inspection, and the customer receives a text with photos and a link to approve the work. AI tools apply to the writing that surrounds each repair order: the inspection notes, the customer explanation, and the follow-up.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review at the shop. We watch how a vehicle moves from check-in to invoice, read the templates the advisors use, and deliver a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation usually means setting up Claude, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Google Gemini with the shop's own inspection forms, labor guide, and warranty language, then automating the customer-facing writing and the end-of-month reporting. We finish by training the front counter on the shop's own repair orders.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An assistant that turns a technician's inspection notes into a plain-language explanation the service advisor can text to the customer.
  • A setup that drafts the recommended-services paragraph on each estimate from the digital vehicle inspection results.
  • A review-reply process that drafts responses to Google and Yelp reviews from the shop's repair order history.
  • A weekly report that summarizes car count, average repair order, and declined work from the shop management system export.
  • A written guide for service advisors on using Claude or Copilot with the shop's own estimate and warranty templates.
