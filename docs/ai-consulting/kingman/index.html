AI consulting in Kingman

Kingman sits at the junction of I-40 and US 93 in Mohave County, and businesses there include health care providers, retail stores, trucking and distribution companies, small manufacturers, and the motels, restaurants, and shops that serve Route 66 travelers. Many of these businesses run on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, QuickBooks for accounting, and an industry system such as a dispatch program, practice management software, or a point of sale. AI consulting applies wherever staff spend time writing, summarizing, or re-entering information those systems already hold.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review at the business, on site in Kingman. We document how work moves through the office, identify the tasks where an AI tool saves time, and deliver a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation means setting up Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude with the company's own documents and templates, automating document preparation and customer communication, and training staff at $1,000 per person with a written guide.
