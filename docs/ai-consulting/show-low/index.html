AI consulting in Show Low

Businesses in Show Low include medical and dental practices, contractors and trades, retail stores, and the restaurants and lodging that serve visitors to the White Mountains. Many of them run on QuickBooks, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, an industry system such as ServiceTitan, Dentrix, or Toast, and a set of Word and Excel documents that staff fill out by hand. AI consulting applies wherever those documents and customer messages take staff time to produce.

Creosote Labs serves businesses in Show Low and the surrounding White Mountains. An engagement starts with a one-day operations review, on site, that documents how the business currently runs and identifies where AI tools would save time. The written assessment includes a quote for each recommendation. Implementation means setting up the tools the business already pays for, such as Copilot, Gemini, or Claude, with its own templates and documents, and then training staff on their own work.

What we do

  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An operations review of a Show Low contractor's office that maps how estimates, change orders, and invoices move from the field to QuickBooks.
