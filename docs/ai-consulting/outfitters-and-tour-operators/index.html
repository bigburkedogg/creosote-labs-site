AI consulting for outfitters and tour operators

Outfitters and tour operators typically take reservations through a booking platform such as FareHarbor, Peek Pro, or Xola, collect waivers through a tool like Smartwaiver, and keep guide schedules, trip manifests, and gear lists in Google Sheets. Guest questions arrive by email and phone, and permit reports, confirmation emails, and packing lists are written by hand each season. AI consulting applies to the writing, lookup, and reporting work that surrounds the booking system rather than the booking system itself.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review at the business, following a trip from inquiry to post-trip follow-up and noting where staff retype the same information. The written assessment lists each place an AI tool fits, with a quote for each. Implementation usually means setting up Claude, Gemini, or Copilot with the company's own trip descriptions, waiver language, and email templates, then training office staff and lead guides to use it for their actual work.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A drafting setup that answers common guest questions about trip difficulty, what to bring, and cancellation terms from the company's own trip pages and policies.
  • A pre-trip email assistant that produces confirmation, packing list, and meeting-point messages for each departure from the booking record.
  • A permit reporting workflow that assembles the season's use figures from FareHarbor exports into the format the land agency requests.
  • A guide briefing generator that turns the trip manifest and guest notes into a one-page sheet for the lead guide.
  • A review response process that drafts replies to Google and TripAdvisor reviews in the company's voice for the owner to approve.
