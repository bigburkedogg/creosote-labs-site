AI consulting for e-commerce businesses

Many e-commerce businesses run on Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce for the storefront, Amazon Seller Central for marketplace sales, ShipStation for fulfillment, Klaviyo for email, and QuickBooks for the books. The daily work between those tools is mostly writing and reading: product descriptions, customer service replies, supplier emails, return decisions, and weekly sales summaries. AI consulting applies to that writing and reading work, and to the reporting that pulls numbers from several tools into one view for the owner.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of how orders, support, and product content move through the business. The written assessment names the tasks where an AI tool saves time and quotes each recommendation. We then set up the tool the business already pays for, such as Claude, Google Gemini, or Microsoft 365 Copilot, with its product catalog, brand voice notes, and return policy, and train staff to use it on their own work.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A product description workflow that drafts Shopify and Amazon listings from a supplier spec sheet and the brand's style guide.
  • A customer service assistant that drafts replies to order status, return, and exchange tickets using the store's actual policies.
  • A weekly sales and ad-spend summary assembled from Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, and Meta Ads exports into a short written report.
  • A review response process that reads new product reviews and drafts replies for a staff member to approve.
  • A supplier correspondence setup that turns purchase order changes and shipping delays into consistent, ready-to-send emails.
