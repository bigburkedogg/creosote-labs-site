AI consulting for marketing agencies

Marketing agencies often run client work across a project tool such as Asana, ClickUp, or Monday.com, with proposals and creative briefs in Google Docs, ad accounts in Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager, and monthly reporting pulled from Google Analytics into slide decks. Much of the writing an agency produces each week, including briefs, campaign summaries, and status updates, follows a repeatable format. AI consulting applies to that repeatable writing and to the reporting that sits behind it.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of how the agency takes on a client, runs a campaign, and reports on it. The review produces a written assessment of where an AI tool the agency already has, such as Google Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Claude, can draft first versions of briefs and reports from the agency's own templates. Each recommendation comes with a quote, and staff are trained on their own accounts and documents.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A monthly client report drafted from Google Analytics and ad platform exports using the agency's report template.
  • A creative brief generator that turns a kickoff call transcript into a brief in the agency's format.
  • A proposal first draft assembled from the agency's past scopes of work and the prospect's intake notes.
  • A weekly client status email drafted from completed tasks in Asana or ClickUp.
  • A review of which AI tools the agency already pays for and how each should be set up for account teams.
