AI consulting for hotels and inns

Many hotels and inns run on a property management system such as Cloudbeds, Little Hotelier, or innRoad, with a channel manager pushing rates and availability to Booking.com, Expedia, and Airbnb. Around that core sit guest emails, group and event inquiries, housekeeping checklists, maintenance logs, and QuickBooks for the books. AI consulting applies to the writing and reading work that surrounds those systems: answering guest questions, preparing confirmations and group proposals, summarizing reviews, and pulling occupancy and revenue figures into a report the owner can read.

Creosote Labs begins with a one-day operations review at the property. We document how reservations, guest communication, housekeeping, and reporting currently work, then deliver a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation typically means setting up the AI tool the property already has access to, such as Google Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Claude, with its own confirmation templates, policies, and rate sheets, and training front desk and management staff to use it on their own work.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A guest inquiry assistant that drafts replies to email and booking-platform messages from the property's own policies, room descriptions, and rate sheet.
  • A group and event proposal generator that produces a room block quote and a proposal letter from a short inquiry form.
  • A review summary that reads new Google, TripAdvisor, and Booking.com reviews each week and reports recurring complaints and compliments by department.
  • A monthly owner report that pulls occupancy, average daily rate, and revenue from the property management system export and writes the narrative.
  • A written guide for front desk staff covering which guest messages the AI tool may draft and which require a manager.
