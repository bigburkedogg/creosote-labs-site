AI consulting in Prescott

Prescott's businesses include medical and dental practices, hotels and restaurants, retail stores, contractors, and professional firms. Many of these businesses already pay for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, keep customer records in a CRM such as HubSpot or an industry system such as Dentrix, Jobber, or Toast, and produce the same documents every week: estimates, intake forms, confirmation emails, monthly reports. AI tools apply best to that repeatable document and communication work.

Creosote Labs serves businesses in Prescott and across Northern Arizona. An engagement starts with a one-day operations review at $3,000. We document how the business currently runs, identify where Copilot, Gemini, or Claude can save staff time, and deliver a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation sets those tools up with the company's own templates and documents, and training is $1,000 per person.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.

Common projects

  • A setup in Microsoft 365 Copilot that drafts patient recall letters and referral summaries from a clinic's own templates.
