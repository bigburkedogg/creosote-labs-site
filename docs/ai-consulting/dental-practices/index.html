AI consulting for dental practices

Many dental practices run on a practice management system such as Dentrix, Open Dental, or Eaglesoft, with QuickBooks for accounting and a mix of Word documents, email, and paper forms around the edges. Front desk staff spend much of the day on insurance verification, treatment plan follow-up, recall reminders, and correspondence with specialists. AI consulting applies to that surrounding work: the letters, summaries, and follow-up that the practice management system does not write on its own.

We spend a day in the office documenting how the front desk, hygienists, and dentists handle each step from scheduling to collections, then deliver a written assessment of where AI tools save time and a quote for each recommendation. Implementation means setting up the tool the practice already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the practice's own templates, then training staff on their own work with a written guide to keep.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A referral letter template that drafts a letter to an oral surgeon or endodontist from the notes in the patient's chart.
  • An assistant set up to write insurance appeal letters from a denied claim and the clinical notes that support it.
  • A recall outreach process that drafts personalized reminders for overdue hygiene patients, ready for the front desk to review and send.
  • A treatment plan summary written in plain language for the patient, generated from the coded plan in Dentrix or Open Dental.
  • A monthly production and collections summary drafted from practice management reports for the owner dentist.
