AI consulting for real estate agents

Real estate agents typically work out of the MLS, a transaction platform such as dotloop, SkySlope, or Lone Wolf Transactions, an e-signature service like DocuSign, and a CRM such as Follow Up Boss or kvCORE. Around those sit the documents that fill an agent's week: comparative market analyses, listing presentations, property descriptions, disclosures, and closing packets. Much of that writing and data entry is repetitive, which is where AI consulting applies.

Creosote Labs assesses how an agent's business currently runs and identifies where AI tools save time. We set up the tools the agent already has access to, such as Google Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Claude, with the agent's own templates, past listings, and standard disclosures. We then automate document preparation, client communication, and reporting, and train the agent and any staff on their own work, with a written guide to keep.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A listing description generator that drafts copy from the MLS input sheet and the agent's past listings.
  • A CMA summary that turns comparable sales data into a client-ready explanation of the recommended price.
  • A follow-up drafting setup inside the agent's CRM that writes check-in messages to past clients and open leads.
  • A transaction email assistant that drafts status updates to buyers, sellers, lenders, and escrow at each milestone.
  • A weekly pipeline report assembled from the CRM and the transaction platform.
