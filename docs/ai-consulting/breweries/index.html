AI consulting for breweries and taprooms

Many breweries run production in a brewery management platform such as Ekos or Ollie, sell across the bar through a taproom point of sale such as Arryved, Toast, or Square, and keep the books in QuickBooks. Around those systems sit the documents the business writes by hand: brew sheets, keg and inventory counts, distributor order forms, the monthly TTB Brewer's Report of Operations, state excise filings, tap lists, and event contracts. AI consulting applies to that written and repeated work.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review at the brewery. We document how beer moves from the brewhouse to the tap list, the distributor, and the books, then deliver a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation usually means setting up the AI tool the brewery already has access to, such as Google Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Claude, with its own recipes, price sheets, and templates, and training staff on that setup.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An assistant that drafts beer descriptions for the tap list, menu, and Untappd from the brewer's recipe notes.
  • A setup that reads distributor sales reports and produces a monthly summary by account and SKU.
  • A prompt library for taproom managers that turns an event inquiry into a quote and a private event contract.
  • A document assistant that pulls monthly production, removals, and inventory figures into a draft TTB Brewer's Report of Operations for review before filing.
  • A written guide for bar staff on answering common customer emails about tours, growler fills, and merchandise.
