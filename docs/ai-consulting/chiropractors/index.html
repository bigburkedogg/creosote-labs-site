AI consulting for chiropractic offices

Many chiropractic offices run patient records and scheduling in ChiroTouch, Jane App, or ChiroFusion, keep the books in QuickBooks, and handle everything else in email and Google Sheets. New-patient intake, SOAP notes, care plan letters, insurance claims, and appointment reminders each take staff time that does not depend on the software. AI consulting looks at where tools like Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude can take on parts of that work without changing the practice management system the office already uses.

We start with a 1-day operations review on site, documenting how the front desk, the treating doctors, and billing currently work. The written assessment identifies the tasks where an AI tool saves real time, such as drafting narrative reports for personal injury cases, summarizing exam findings into patient-friendly care plans, or answering common scheduling emails, with a quote for each. We then set up the chosen tool with the office's own templates and train staff to use it.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A Claude or Copilot setup that drafts personal injury narrative reports from the doctor's exam notes and the office's report template.
  • An assistant that turns a completed re-exam into a plain-language progress summary for the patient.
  • Drafted replies to common front-desk emails, such as insurance coverage questions and reschedule requests, for staff to review and send.
  • A monthly reporting routine that summarizes visit counts, no-shows, and collections from ChiroTouch exports into a short owner's report.
  • A written guide for staff covering which tasks go to the AI tool and which stay in the practice management system.
