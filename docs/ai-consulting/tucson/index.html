AI consulting in Tucson

Many Tucson businesses are contractors, medical and dental practices, law and accounting firms, restaurants, and property managers, along with the manufacturers and service companies that supply larger employers. Businesses of these kinds often already pay for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and many keep their records in QuickBooks, a spreadsheet, or an industry system such as ServiceTitan, Dentrix, or AppFolio. AI consulting applies where staff spend hours each week preparing documents, answering the same customer emails, and assembling reports from those systems.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of a Tucson business, on site, and delivers a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. We then set up the AI tool the company already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with its own templates and documents, automate the document preparation and reporting identified in the review, and train staff on their own work. The team receives a written guide it can follow after we leave.
