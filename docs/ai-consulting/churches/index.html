AI consulting for churches and religious organizations

Many churches run their office on a church management system such as Planning Center, Breeze, or ChurchTrac for members and giving, QuickBooks or Aplos for accounting, and Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for email and documents. The week's work includes bulletins, sermon notes, newsletters, giving statements, volunteer schedules, and meeting minutes. Much of that is repetitive writing and reformatting that AI tools such as Gemini, Copilot, or Claude can handle once they are set up with the church's own templates.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of the church office. We document how announcements, giving records, and pastoral follow-up currently move between staff and volunteers, then identify where an AI tool saves time. From there we set up the tool the church already has access to with its own bulletin format, letter templates, and policies, automate the recurring documents, and train staff on their own work. The review produces a written assessment and a quote for each recommendation.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A bulletin and announcement draft produced each week from the calendar in Planning Center and the church's standard layout.
  • Year-end giving statements generated from the giving system and merged into the church's letter template.
  • A first draft of the monthly newsletter assembled from notes submitted by ministry leaders.
  • Meeting minutes for elder or council meetings drafted from a recording and filed in the church's Google Drive.
  • Standard replies for facility rental, baptism, and wedding inquiries drafted from the church's written policies.
