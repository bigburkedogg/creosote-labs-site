AI consulting for cleaning companies

Many cleaning companies schedule and dispatch through Jobber, Housecall Pro, or ZenMaid, invoice through QuickBooks, and keep the rest in Google Sheets and text messages. Recurring residential clients, one-time deep cleans, and commercial contracts each produce their own paperwork: walkthrough notes, cleaning checklists, quotes, and monthly invoices. Office staff often spend part of each day writing the same emails and re-entering the same information from one tool into another.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of how the company books, schedules, cleans, and bills. We identify where AI tools save time, such as drafting quotes from walkthrough notes, answering routine customer emails, or summarizing crew feedback from the field. We then set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Google Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Claude, with the company's own checklists and templates, and train office staff to use them.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An assistant that drafts a quote email from a completed walkthrough form and the company's price list.
  • A setup in Gemini or Copilot that answers customer questions about scheduling, cancellations, and what a service includes, using the company's own policy documents.
  • A weekly summary for the owner built from missed-clean reports and complaint emails.
  • A process for turning voice notes from crew leads into written job notes in the customer record.
  • Templates that generate commercial cleaning proposals from a site survey and square footage.
