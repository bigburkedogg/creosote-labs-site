AI consulting for solopreneurs and independent professionals

A one-person business usually runs on a handful of general-purpose tools rather than an industry system. Many solopreneurs keep clients and invoices in QuickBooks, FreshBooks, or Wave, book appointments through Calendly or Acuity, write proposals in Google Docs or Word, and track everything else in Gmail and Google Sheets. The owner handles sales, delivery, and administration alone, so any task that repeats every week, such as drafting proposals, answering the same inquiry, or writing up notes after a call, is a candidate for an AI tool.

Creosote Labs begins with a one-day operations review at $3,000. We walk through how the business takes on a client, delivers the work, and gets paid, then provide a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation typically means setting up Claude, Google Gemini, or Microsoft 365 Copilot with the owner's own proposal templates, service descriptions, and past correspondence, so the tool produces drafts in the owner's voice. We finish with a written guide covering each setup, so the process holds up without us.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A proposal drafting setup that produces a first draft from a discovery call transcript and the owner's past proposals.
  • An inquiry response workflow that drafts replies to new leads from a set of approved answers about services and pricing.
  • A call-notes process that turns a recording or rough notes into a client summary and a list of next steps.
  • A monthly reporting routine that pulls invoice data from QuickBooks or FreshBooks into a short written summary for the owner.
  • A written guide documenting each AI setup so the owner can repeat it and update it as the business changes.
