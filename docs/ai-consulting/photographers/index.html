AI consulting for photography businesses

Many photography businesses run on a client management tool such as HoneyBook, Dubsado, Studio Ninja, or Táve for inquiries, contracts, and invoices, with Lightroom and Photoshop for editing and Pixieset or ShootProof for gallery delivery. Inquiry replies, pricing guides, session prep emails, and blog posts are typically written by hand, one at a time. AI tools such as Claude, Google Gemini, or Microsoft 365 Copilot apply to that writing and to the sorting, tagging, and reporting around it.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of how the studio books, shoots, edits, and delivers. The written assessment identifies where AI tools save time, with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation sets up the tool the studio already has access to with its own pricing guide, contract templates, and email sequences, so the work it produces matches what the studio already sends. Staff training on the studio's own work is available, with a written guide for the team.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An inquiry-response assistant that drafts replies from the studio's pricing guide and availability calendar.
  • A session prep email generator that fills the studio's what-to-wear and location templates from booking details in HoneyBook.
  • A gallery delivery email and blog post drafted from the shoot's date, location, and selected images.
  • A tagging workflow that suggests keywords and captions for Lightroom exports before they are uploaded to the website.
  • A monthly report that summarizes bookings, revenue by session type, and open invoices from QuickBooks.
