AI consulting for law firms

Many small law firms run on a practice management system such as Clio, MyCase, or PracticePanther for matters, time entries, and billing, with Microsoft Word and Outlook for drafting and correspondence. Engagement letters, demand letters, discovery responses, and client intake forms tend to start from templates and get finished by hand. AI tools apply best to that drafting and review work, and to the routine communication that surrounds every matter.

Creosote Labs begins with a one-day operations review of the firm. We document how matters move from intake to closing, identify where an AI tool the firm already licenses, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot or Claude, can take on first drafts and summaries, and deliver a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. Implementation covers setting up the tool with the firm's own templates and precedent documents, and training attorneys and staff on their own files.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A Copilot or Claude setup loaded with the firm's engagement letter, fee agreement, and demand letter templates so a first draft comes from the firm's own language.
  • A review of a personal injury practice's intake-to-demand workflow with a written list of the steps an AI tool can draft and the steps that stay with the attorney.
  • A summarization routine for deposition transcripts and medical records that produces a timeline and a page-cited summary for attorney review.
  • A client communication assistant that drafts status updates from matter notes in Clio for a paralegal to check and send.
  • A written assessment of where confidentiality rules limit which documents may be sent to a hosted AI tool, with the firm's policy documented for staff.
