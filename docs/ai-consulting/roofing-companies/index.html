AI consulting for roofing companies

Many roofing companies run on a job management platform such as AccuLynx, JobNimbus, or Roofr, with QuickBooks for accounting, CompanyCam for job photos, and aerial measurement reports from EagleView or Hover. Around those tools sits a large amount of written work: estimates, insurance supplement letters, material orders, warranty documents, and follow-up with homeowners and adjusters. AI tools apply most directly to that written work, and to pulling information out of the photos, reports, and emails a job produces.

Creosote Labs starts with a one-day operations review of how a roofing company estimates, sells, schedules, and closes out jobs. The written assessment identifies where AI tools save time, such as drafting supplement requests from an adjuster's scope, summarizing inspection photos into a homeowner report, or turning a signed contract into a material order. We then set up the tool the company already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with its own templates, and train office staff to use it.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • A prompt and template set that drafts an insurance supplement letter from the adjuster's estimate and the crew's job photos.
  • An assistant that reads an EagleView or Hover report and fills in the company's estimate template with the measured squares, pitch, and waste factor.
  • A written homeowner inspection report generated from CompanyCam photos and the inspector's notes.
  • A review of the sales and production process with a ranked list of where AI tools apply and a quote for each.
  • A follow-up email workflow that drafts messages to homeowners at each stage of a job, from inspection through final invoice.
