AI consulting for manufacturers

Small manufacturers often run on QuickBooks for accounting, Excel for bills of materials and job costing, and a shop management system such as JobBOSS, Fishbowl, or Katana for work orders and inventory. Around those systems sits a large amount of written work: RFQ responses, purchase orders to suppliers, certificates of conformance, inspection reports, and email that goes back and forth with customers about lead times and change requests. AI consulting applies to that written work and to the reporting that pulls numbers from several systems at once.

Creosote Labs spends a day in the shop and the front office documenting how quotes, orders, and shipments move through the business, then delivers a written assessment of where an AI tool the company already has, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot or Claude, saves time. We set the tool up with the company's own quote templates, part drawings, and supplier records, automate the drafting of RFQ responses and customer updates, and train staff on real jobs. The review is $3,000, and each recommendation comes with its own quote.

What we do

  • Assess current processes and identify where AI tools save time.
  • Set up the tools the business already has access to, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude, with the company's own documents and templates.
  • Automate document preparation, customer communication, and reporting.
  • Train staff on the company's own work, with a written guide for the team.

Common projects

  • An assistant set up in Microsoft 365 Copilot that drafts RFQ responses from the company's quote history and standard terms.
  • A workflow that turns a customer's emailed drawing and quantity into a structured quote request for the estimator to price.
  • Automated drafting of certificates of conformance and inspection summaries from the data already recorded on the traveler.
  • A weekly production report that pulls open orders, late jobs, and material shortages from JobBOSS and QuickBooks into one summary.
  • Staff training on using Claude with the company's own work instructions and supplier correspondence.
