AI and operations consulting for small businesses.

Creosote Labs helps owner-run businesses replace spreadsheets and manual processes with systems that fit how they operate, builds the websites and applications they need, and trains teams to use AI tools in their day-to-day work.

Services

  • Operations review

    We spend a day on site with your team, document how your business currently runs, and deliver a written assessment with prioritized recommendations and a quote for each.

  • Systems and automation

    We consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into a single system, build the internal tools your business needs, and automate repetitive work.

  • Websites and web applications

    We build marketing websites, customer portals, and web applications, from a site with clear services and pricing to a booking or quoting tool your customers use directly.

  • Team AI training

    A one-day, hands-on workshop delivered on your team's own tools, documents, and templates. Every participant leaves with a working setup and a written guide. Details
