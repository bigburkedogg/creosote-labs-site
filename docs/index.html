Creosote Labs is a research organization working on applied AI: what these systems do once they are deployed into a working business.

We build and run systems in production, publish notes on what we find, and develop products of our own.

01

What we work on

  1. 1.1

    Operations systems

    Software that runs scheduling, inventory and logistics for businesses whose work is booked in advance and carried out in the field. A change entered once, such as a guest's dietary restriction, reaches every list that depends on it.

  2. 1.2

    Document tooling

    Tools that pull structure out of documents laid out for people rather than machines: contracts, agency forms and flat PDF packets. The output includes the page and position of each field, down to every signature and initial line in a contract packet.

  3. 1.3

    Teaching applications

    Applications that turn a fixed body of source material into something a specific reader can work through. The work includes placing the reader at the right level and checking what they learned, with placement exams, graded answers and spaced review.

  4. 1.4

    Assessment and course engines for practitioners

    Software that runs an assessment on a practitioner's website, sends the email that follows it and sells the practitioner's courses. The assessment brings in new contacts, and course payments run through Stripe.
