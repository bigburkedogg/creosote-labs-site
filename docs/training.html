Training

Team AI training

A one-day, hands-on workshop delivered on your team's own tools, documents, and templates. Designed for teams of 5 to 20 people who work primarily in email, documents, and spreadsheets.

Format

  • Delivered in person at your office, or remotely for smaller groups.
  • Runs in the AI tool your company already uses: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Gemini, or Claude.
  • Participants work on their own computers with their own documents and templates.
  • One department per session. Managers attend with their teams.
  • Up to 20 participants per session. Larger teams are scheduled as consecutive sessions.

What's included

  • A planning call and a one-page IT checklist before the session.
  • The full-day workshop.
  • A written guide for the team, covering everything built during the day.
  • A 30-day follow-up session.
  • follow-up session: remote or on site

Agenda

  • 8:30Setup. Confirming accounts, tools, and sample documents on each participant's computer.
  • 9:00Introduction. What the tool does and does not do, and your company's rules for what data it can be used with.
  • 9:45Workflow 1. Each participant produces a document they create regularly, from their own template.
  • 11:15Workflow 2. A recurring task involving data: cleaning up a spreadsheet, summarizing a report, drafting a set of replies.
  • 12:30Lunch.
  • 1:15Working instructions. Each participant documents their own process so the tool can follow it consistently.
  • 2:30Workflow 3, or a session for managers on where the tools fit in the team's week.
  • 3:30Review of the written guide. Agreement on a 30-day measure. Scheduling of the follow-up session.

Requirements

  • Company accounts and licenses for the chosen AI tool for each participant. Licenses are not included in the price; the IT checklist lists the plan each tool requires.
  • Three sample documents per participant, placed in a folder on your company's own storage before the session.
  • A meeting room with a computer for each participant, and lunch.

Data and security

  • All work is done on your computers, in your company's accounts. Creosote Labs keeps no copies of your documents.
  • Data is sent only to the AI tool vendor your company has already approved.
  • Nothing is installed on staff computers unless your IT department chooses a desktop version of the tool.
  • A non-disclosure agreement is available on request.
