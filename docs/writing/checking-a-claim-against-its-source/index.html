A claim gets made in public, it cites a paper, and the citation turns out to be doing more work than the paper supports. The steps below are the ones we use: quote the source verbatim with a page or section number, link both the claim and the source so a reader can check the work, and finish with the narrower statement the source does support rather than only with the gap.
