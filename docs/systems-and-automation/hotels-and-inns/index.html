Systems and automation for hotels and inns

A small hotel or inn often keeps its records in several places at once: reservations in the property management system, housekeeping assignments on a printed sheet, maintenance requests in text messages, group bookings in Excel, and accounting in QuickBooks. In many properties, rates are updated by hand in the channel manager, and the same guest details are retyped into confirmations, registration cards, and invoices. Systems and automation work applies wherever information is copied between those places or tracked outside the main system.

Creosote Labs consolidates those spreadsheets and side lists into one system of record connected to the property management system, then builds the internal tools the property needs, such as a housekeeping board, a maintenance ticket log, or a group booking tracker. We automate the repetitive steps: pushing nightly arrivals to housekeeping, generating invoices for direct bookings, and sending post-stay follow-up. Every engagement ends with written documentation and a handover to the staff who will run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A housekeeping board that builds each morning's room list from the property management system's departures and arrivals and records when each room is clean and inspected.
  • A maintenance ticket log where staff report a broken fixture from a phone and the owner sees open items, assigned vendors, and cost by room.
  • A group and wedding block tracker that replaces an Excel workbook, holding room lists, deposits, cutoff dates, and the final rooming list in one place.
  • An invoice generator that produces a folio and invoice for direct and corporate bookings from reservation data and posts it to QuickBooks.
  • A nightly report that emails occupancy, revenue, and tomorrow's arrivals to the owner without anyone exporting a spreadsheet.
  • A post-stay sequence that sends a thank-you note and review request after checkout and flags responses that need a reply.
