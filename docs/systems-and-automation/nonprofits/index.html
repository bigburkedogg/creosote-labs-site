Systems and automation for nonprofits

Nonprofits often keep records in more places than they intend to. Donor history sits in Neon CRM or Bloomerang, the finance side in QuickBooks, volunteer sign-ups in Google Sheets or Volgistics, event registrations in Eventbrite, and program outcomes in a spreadsheet each program manager maintains separately. Reconciling gifts against deposits, preparing a grant report, or answering a board question about a program means pulling from several of these by hand.

Creosote Labs consolidates that into one system of record and builds the internal tools around it. A typical project connects the donor database to QuickBooks so gifts and deposits match without a monthly cleanup, replaces the program spreadsheets with a single tracking tool, and automates volunteer reminders, pledge follow-up, and the recurring reports the board and funders expect. We document the system and hand it over to staff.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A program tracking tool that replaces per-program spreadsheets and reports outcomes in the format each funder requires.
  • A gift reconciliation automation that matches donor database entries against QuickBooks deposits and flags the differences.
  • A volunteer scheduling system that handles sign-ups, shift reminders, and hours reporting.
  • A pledge follow-up automation that sends reminders on a schedule and records responses in the donor database.
  • A grant calendar that tracks application deadlines, report due dates, and restricted fund balances in one place.
  • A monthly board dashboard that assembles financials, fundraising totals, and program numbers without manual copying.
