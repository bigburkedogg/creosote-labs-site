Systems and automation for marketing agencies

A marketing agency typically tracks retainers, hours, and deliverables across several places at once: time in Harvest or Toggl, tasks in Monday.com or Asana, invoices in QuickBooks, and a Google Sheet that reconciles what each client was promised against what was delivered. Content calendars, approval rounds, and reporting dates tend to live in separate spreadsheets. Systems and automation work addresses the gaps between those tools and the manual copying that fills them.

Creosote Labs consolidates the agency's client records, scopes, hours, and deliverables into one system of record, then builds the internal tools the agency needs on top of it, such as a retainer tracker that shows hours used against hours sold for every account. We automate the work that repeats each month, including invoice preparation, report scheduling, and approval reminders. The system is documented and handed over to the account and operations staff who run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A retainer tracker that pulls logged hours from Harvest and shows burn against each client's monthly allotment.
  • Monthly invoice preparation in QuickBooks generated from approved time entries and fixed-fee deliverables.
  • A client approval workflow that sends drafts for sign-off and records the response without an email chain.
  • A content calendar shared across accounts that replaces one spreadsheet per client.
  • A capacity dashboard showing which staff are booked and which have room for new work.
  • Automated onboarding that creates the project board, shared folder, and reporting schedule when a contract is signed.
