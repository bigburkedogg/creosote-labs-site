Systems and automation in Camp Verde

Many businesses in Camp Verde run on a mix of Excel or Google Sheets workbooks, a QuickBooks file, and a job or booking system that does not talk to the others. A contractor might track leads in a spreadsheet, quote in Word, and invoice in QuickBooks, entering the same customer three times. A tour operator or lodging business often keeps reservations, waivers, and staff schedules in separate places. Systems and automation work applies wherever the same information is typed more than once.

Creosote Labs works with Camp Verde businesses to consolidate those tools into a single system of record, build the internal tools they need for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting, and automate data entry, follow-up, and document generation. Each project begins with a one-day operations review at $3,000, which produces a written assessment and a fixed quote for every recommendation. Implementation is quoted from that review, and the finished system is documented and handed over to staff.
