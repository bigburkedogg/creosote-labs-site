Systems and automation for dental practices

A dental office typically keeps its clinical and scheduling records in Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental, but a good deal of the operation lives elsewhere. Insurance verification often runs through a spreadsheet, unscheduled treatment is tracked on a printed report, lab cases are logged in a notebook or Google Sheets, and supply orders and payroll hours sit in QuickBooks or Excel. Systems and automation work addresses the gap between the practice management system and everything the team tracks by hand around it.

Creosote Labs starts with an operations review, then builds the internal tools the practice chooses from the quoted recommendations. That might be a single lab case tracker that replaces the notebook, an unscheduled treatment list that updates from the practice management system's reports, or an automated end-of-day routine that reconciles deposits against QuickBooks. Each tool is tested with the front desk, documented in plain language, and handed over so the office runs it without us.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A lab case tracker that logs each crown, denture, or aligner case when it is sent out and flags cases due back before the seat appointment.
  • An insurance verification worksheet that pulls tomorrow's schedule and records eligibility, remaining benefits, and frequency limits in one place.
  • An unscheduled treatment report that lists patients with accepted but unbooked treatment plans and assigns them to a team member for follow-up.
  • A daily reconciliation routine that compares the day sheet from the practice management system with the bank deposit and QuickBooks.
  • A supply ordering list that tracks par levels for operatory stock and produces a purchase order when items run low.
  • A staff hours and PTO tracker that replaces a shared spreadsheet and feeds payroll.
