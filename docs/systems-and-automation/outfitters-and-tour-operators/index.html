Systems and automation for outfitters and tour operators

An outfitter's records often live in several places at once. Reservations sit in Peek Pro or FareHarbor, signed waivers in WaiverSign, guide availability in a shared Google Sheet, gear inventory in another sheet or a paper log, and invoices in QuickBooks. Multi-day trip operators add menus, shopping lists, shuttle schedules, and permit allocations on top. These gaps between systems are where double bookings, stale manifests, and long end-of-season reconciliations tend to come from.

Creosote Labs consolidates that into one system of record. After the operations review, we build the internal tools the business needs: a trip calendar that pulls bookings from the reservation platform, a guide scheduling tool, a gear and vehicle checkout log, or a menu planner that scales shopping lists to headcount. Repetitive work such as manifest generation, waiver status checks, and invoice creation is automated. The system is documented and handed over to staff.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A trip tracker that lists every departure with guests, guides, vehicles, and waiver status in one view, fed from the booking platform.
  • A guide scheduling tool that matches guide certifications and availability to the trip calendar and sends each guide a weekly assignment.
  • A menu and provisioning system for multi-day trips that produces a shopping list and pack-out sheet from the headcount and dietary notes.
  • An automation that flags any booked guest without a signed waiver 48 hours before departure and sends the reminder.
  • A permit and allocation ledger that tracks user days against each agency permit as bookings come in.
  • A gear inventory log with checkout and return by trip, replacing a paper sheet at the warehouse.
