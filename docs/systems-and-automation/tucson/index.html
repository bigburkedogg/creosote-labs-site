Systems and automation in Tucson

Many Tucson businesses run on a mix of tools that were adopted one at a time: QuickBooks for invoicing, Google Sheets or Excel for scheduling and job tracking, a shared inbox for customer requests, and an industry system such as Jobber, Housecall Pro, Clio, or Buildium for part of the work. Contractors, clinics, property managers, and professional firms often re-enter the same customer, job, or client data in 3 or 4 places. Systems and automation applies wherever that duplication exists.

Creosote Labs consolidates those spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record for a Tucson business, builds the internal tools it needs for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting, and automates the data entry, follow-up, and document generation that staff currently do by hand. Work begins with a one-day operations review at $3,000 that produces a written assessment and a quote for each recommendation. Every system we build is documented and handed over to staff.
