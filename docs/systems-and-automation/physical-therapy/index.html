Systems and automation for physical therapy clinics

A physical therapy clinic's EMR handles the visit itself, but much of the clinic's administration lives elsewhere. Referral logs, authorization tracking with visit counts and expiration dates, waitlists, equipment inventories, and marketing follow-up to referring physicians often sit in Google Sheets or Excel, while accounting runs in QuickBooks and billing in a clearinghouse such as Waystar. Front desk staff commonly re-key the same patient details across these places. Systems and automation work addresses the gap between the EMR and everything around it.

Creosote Labs consolidates those spreadsheets into one system of record that the front desk and therapists share, builds the internal tools the clinic needs for tracking authorizations, referrals, and waitlists, and automates the data entry between them. Each project is scoped and quoted from a one-day operations review, so the clinic knows the fixed price before any work begins. When the build is finished, we document the system, train the staff who will run it, and hand it over.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • An authorization tracker that records approved visits per patient, counts each completed visit against the total, and flags cases approaching their limit or expiration date.
  • A referral log that captures each incoming referral, its source, and its scheduling status, replacing a shared spreadsheet.
  • A waitlist tool that matches cancellations to patients waiting for a particular therapist or time slot.
  • An automated end-of-day export from the EMR into QuickBooks so deposits and charges reconcile without manual entry.
  • A referring physician dashboard showing referrals, active cases, and discharges by practice for the clinic's outreach visits.
  • A plan-of-care expiration report that lists patients due for recertification in the next 2 weeks.
