Systems and automation in Show Low

Many businesses in Show Low serve customers from across the White Mountains, and their records tend to be spread across several tools. Trades and construction companies often track work orders in Jobber or Housecall Pro alongside spreadsheets, clinics frequently keep scheduling and billing in separate systems, and retailers and restaurants often reconcile Square or Toast against QuickBooks by hand. Systems and automation work applies wherever the same information is typed into more than one place, or a report is rebuilt by hand each week.

Creosote Labs consolidates those spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record for businesses in Show Low and the White Mountains. Work begins with a one-day operations review at $3,000 that documents current processes and quotes each recommendation. From there we build the internal tools a business needs for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting, automate data entry and follow-up between existing software, and hand the finished system to staff with written documentation.
