Systems and automation for general contractors

A general contracting office often runs a job across several places at once: an estimate in Excel, a schedule in Buildertrend or a spreadsheet, invoices and job costing in QuickBooks, and subcontractor bids, lien waivers, and pay applications in email and shared folders. The same job name, budget line, and contact often get typed into each of them. When the systems do not connect, the office spends time reconciling them, and the owner may have no single place to see where every job stands.

Creosote Labs consolidates those spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record for the company's jobs, then builds the internal tools the office needs on top of it: a quoting tool, a change order tracker, a subcontractor and pay application log, a job cost report. We automate the repetitive steps, such as re-entering data, chasing lien waivers, and generating closeout documents, then document the system and hand it over to the staff who will run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A job tracker that shows every active project, its budget, committed costs, and open change orders in one place.
  • A quoting tool that produces a formatted estimate and proposal from a takeoff spreadsheet.
  • A change order workflow that logs each change, sends it to the owner for approval, and updates the contract value.
  • A subcontractor pay application process that collects lien waivers and certificates of insurance before a check goes out.
  • An automated weekly job cost report built from QuickBooks data instead of a hand-assembled spreadsheet.
  • A closeout package generator that assembles warranties, as-builts, and final lien waivers by project.
