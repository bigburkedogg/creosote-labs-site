Systems and automation for schools and tutoring businesses

A tutoring center or small private school often keeps its student roster in a spreadsheet, its schedule in Google Calendar or a booking tool, its invoices in QuickBooks, and its attendance and progress records in Google Sheets or Excel. Enrollment forms arrive by email or through a Google Form, and someone re-enters each family's details into the other tools by hand. Each system holds part of the picture, and reconciling them at month end is a recurring task.

Creosote Labs builds a single system of record for students, families, schedules, and billing, then automates the work that currently moves between tools. A typical project connects enrollment to scheduling and invoicing so a new student is entered once, generates tuition invoices and reminders on schedule, and produces attendance and progress reports without a spreadsheet export. We document the system and hand it over to the office staff who will run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A student and family database that replaces the roster spreadsheet and feeds scheduling, billing, and reporting from one record.
  • An enrollment workflow that takes a completed online form and creates the student record, schedule slot, and first invoice automatically.
  • A tutor scheduling tool that matches availability to student requests and sends confirmations and reminders to families.
  • Automated tuition invoicing and payment reminders that post to QuickBooks and flag past-due accounts for the office.
  • An attendance and progress tracker tutors update after each session, with a report families receive at the end of each term.
  • A make-up session and cancellation log that applies the center's policy and adjusts billing without manual review.
