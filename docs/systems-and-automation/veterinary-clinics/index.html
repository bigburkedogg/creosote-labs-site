Systems and automation for veterinary clinics

A veterinary clinic usually keeps its medical records and invoicing in one practice management system, such as Cornerstone, AVImark, or ezyVet, and everything else in spreadsheets: the controlled-substance log, the boarding calendar, vendor orders, callbacks after surgery, and the list of overdue vaccination reminders. Front desk staff often re-enter the same client information into the reminder service, the payment terminal, and the email tool. Systems and automation work addresses that gap between the practice management system and the spreadsheets around it.

Creosote Labs consolidates those side spreadsheets into one system of record, builds the internal tools a clinic needs for scheduling, inventory, and callback tracking, and automates the data entry between them. A typical project exports appointment and invoice data from the practice management system on a schedule, keeps a single client list current across the reminder and payment tools, and generates the reports the practice manager assembles by hand each month. We document the system and hand it to staff.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A post-surgery callback tracker that lists every patient discharged that day and records when the technician reached the owner.
  • An inventory and reorder tool that tracks vaccine and medication stock against usage and prepares the weekly vendor order.
  • A boarding and grooming calendar that checks vaccination status from the practice management export before a reservation is confirmed.
  • A monthly reporting dashboard that pulls production by doctor, revenue by service category, and accounts receivable from the practice management system and QuickBooks.
  • A controlled-substance log that replaces the paper binder with a searchable record reconciled against dispensing.
