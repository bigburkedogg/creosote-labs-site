Systems and automation in Sedona

Tour operators, lodging properties, restaurants, galleries, and property managers are common in Sedona, and many of them keep records in several places at once. A typical setup is a reservation platform, QuickBooks, a shared inbox, and a set of Excel or Google Sheets files for schedules, inventory, vendor lists, and commissions. Systems and automation work applies when the same information is typed into more than one of those places by hand.

Creosote Labs works with businesses in Sedona and across Northern Arizona. The first step is a one-day operations review at $3,000, which produces a written assessment and a quoted recommendation for each item. From there we consolidate the spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record, build the internal tools the business needs for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting, automate the repetitive entry and follow-up, and document everything before handing it to staff.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A trip-scheduling board for a jeep or hiking tour company that assigns guides and vehicles and replaces a shared spreadsheet.
  • A gallery inventory system that tracks consignment pieces, artist commissions, and sales in one place instead of a binder and a spreadsheet.
  • A vendor and maintenance work order tracker for an inn that logs requests, assigns them, and records completion.
  • An automated owner statement for a vacation rental manager built from booking and expense data.
  • A wedding and event booking pipeline that moves inquiries from the website into a tracker with automatic follow-up.
  • A guide payroll and tip reconciliation tool for a tour operator that reads the trip schedule and produces the figures the bookkeeper enters into QuickBooks.
