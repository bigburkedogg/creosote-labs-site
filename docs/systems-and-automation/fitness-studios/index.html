Systems and automation for gyms and fitness studios

A fitness studio's records often sit in several places at once: class bookings and memberships in Mindbody, WellnessLiving, or Pike13, trainer hours in a shared Google Sheet, payroll in Gusto or QuickBooks, leads from the website in a Mailchimp list, and personal-training packages tracked in a notebook at the front desk. Reconciling those sources each month, chasing failed payments, and following up with trial members are manual jobs that the platform does not do on its own.

Creosote Labs consolidates those sources into one system of record and automates the work around it. A typical project connects the membership platform's exports to a single dashboard, builds a trainer scheduling and payroll tool that replaces the spreadsheet, and sets up automated follow-up for trials, missed sessions, and declined cards. We document the system and hand it to the front desk staff who will run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A trainer payroll tool that calculates session pay from class and personal-training records instead of a monthly spreadsheet.
  • Automated follow-up sequences for trial visits, expiring packages, and failed membership payments.
  • A single dashboard that pulls attendance, revenue, and churn from the membership platform and QuickBooks.
  • A lead tracker that captures website and walk-in inquiries and assigns them to staff for follow-up.
  • An equipment maintenance log with scheduled reminders for service dates.
  • A waiver and intake process that files signed forms against the member record automatically.
