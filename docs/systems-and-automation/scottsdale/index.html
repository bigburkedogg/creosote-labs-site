Systems and automation in Scottsdale

Many Scottsdale businesses in hospitality, healthcare, real estate, and financial services run on a mix of a core platform and a set of spreadsheets that grew up around it. A property management company might keep leases in AppFolio and vendor work orders in Excel; a medical practice might schedule in its EHR and track referrals in Google Sheets; a tour operator might take bookings online and manage guides and vehicles on a whiteboard. The gaps between those tools are where data gets re-entered and follow-up gets missed.

Creosote Labs works with Scottsdale businesses to replace that mix with one system of record. We spend a day on site documenting the current process, then deliver a written assessment with a quote for each recommendation. From there we consolidate the spreadsheets, build the internal tools the business needs for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting, and automate data entry, follow-up, and document generation. Every system is documented and handed over to staff so it can be run without us.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A vendor work order tracker for a property management company that replaces an Excel log and syncs unit and owner data from AppFolio.
  • A trip roster and guide scheduling tool for a tour operator that pulls confirmed bookings into one calendar and generates the day's manifest.
  • A referral tracking system for a specialty medical practice that logs incoming referrals, assigns follow-up, and reports turnaround time.
  • An automated proposal generator for a landscaping company that builds an estimate and contract from a site visit form and pushes the job to QuickBooks.
  • A client onboarding workflow for a financial advisory practice that collects documents, populates the CRM, and produces the account opening packet.
