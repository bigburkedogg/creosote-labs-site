Systems and automation in Payson

Contractors, clinics, and retail stores in Payson tend to accumulate tools over time. A contractor might track jobs in Excel, invoice from QuickBooks, and schedule crews by text message. A clinic might keep its practice management system, a separate spreadsheet for referrals, and paper intake forms. Systems and automation work applies when the same information lives in several of those places and someone has to keep them in agreement by hand, usually at the end of the day or the end of the month.

Creosote Labs works with Payson and Rim Country businesses to consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record, build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting, and automate data entry, follow-up, and document generation. Every system is documented and handed over to the staff who will run it. Pricing begins with a one-day operations review at $3,000, which produces a written assessment and a quote for each recommendation; implementation is quoted from that review, with no hourly billing.

Common projects

  • A job tracker for a general contractor that replaces a shared Excel workbook and pushes completed jobs to QuickBooks for invoicing.
