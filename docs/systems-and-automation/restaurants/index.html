Systems and automation for restaurants

Restaurants typically keep their operating data in several places at once. Sales live in the POS, whether Toast, Square, or Clover. Labor lives in 7shifts or HotSchedules. Vendor invoices from Sysco, US Foods, and local suppliers arrive on paper or by email, inventory counts are taken on clipboards or in Google Sheets, and the bookkeeper reconciles all of it in QuickBooks at month end. Systems and automation work applies wherever those records are copied by hand from one place to another.

Creosote Labs consolidates those records into one system of record and builds the tools around it. A project might connect POS sales exports, labor hours, and vendor invoices into a single daily report, replace the inventory clipboard with a counting tool that generates the order guide, or build a catering tracker that carries an event from inquiry to invoice. Each engagement starts with a one-day operations review, and every system is documented and handed over to the managers who will run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A daily flash report that pulls sales from the POS and hours from the scheduling system into one page for the owner each morning.
  • An inventory count tool for phones and tablets that produces the vendor order guide and flags items below par.
  • A vendor invoice pipeline that reads emailed PDFs from suppliers and enters line items into QuickBooks for the bookkeeper to approve.
  • A catering and private-event tracker that holds the inquiry, menu, deposit, event sheet, and final invoice in one record.
  • A recipe and plate-cost database that recalculates food cost when supplier prices change.
  • A prep list generator that builds each station's list from the day's reservations and the par sheet.
