Systems and automation in Prescott

Businesses in Prescott include hospitals and medical practices, hotels and restaurants serving visitors, contractors, property managers, and downtown shops. Many of them track work in a mix of Excel or Google Sheets, QuickBooks, and a field or booking tool such as ServiceTitan, AppFolio, or Square, with the gaps between those systems filled by re-typing. Scheduling, quoting, job tracking, and month-end reporting are the areas where that re-typing accumulates.

Creosote Labs consolidates those spreadsheets and disconnected tools into a single system of record for businesses in Prescott and the surrounding area. Engagements begin with a one-day operations review at $3,000, which produces a written assessment and a fixed quote for each recommendation. Common projects

  • A single job board that replaces a roofing company's 3 scheduling spreadsheets and pushes completed jobs into QuickBooks.
  • A work-order tracker for a property management company that logs tenant requests, assigns vendors, and records the invoice against the unit.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does a Prescott business have to replace QuickBooks or its booking software?

    Usually not. Projects typically keep the accounting and booking tools already in use and connect them, replacing only the spreadsheets and manual steps in between.

  • What happens to the existing spreadsheet data?

    We import it into the new system during implementation and check it against the originals before staff switch over. Nothing is discarded until the business confirms the new records are correct.
