Systems and automation for roofing companies

A roofing company's information often lives in several places at once: leads in a spreadsheet or a CRM, estimates in AccuLynx or JobNimbus, measurements in EagleView reports, photos in CompanyCam, crew schedules on a whiteboard or in Google Sheets, and invoices in QuickBooks. Each handoff between those places is a manual step. Job details are retyped from the estimate into the material order, from the material order into the schedule, and from the completed job into the invoice, and the same homeowner is often contacted from more than one system.

Creosote Labs consolidates those pieces into one system of record and builds the internal tools around it: a job board that shows every roof from lead to final payment, a quoting tool that produces an estimate from a measurement report, and a production calendar tied to material deliveries. Where a step is repeated on every job, we automate it, so an accepted estimate creates the material order and the QuickBooks invoice without retyping. The system is documented and handed over to the office staff who will run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A job tracking board that carries each roof from inspection through insurance approval, build day, and final payment in one screen.
  • An estimating tool that takes squares, pitch, and material selections and produces a priced proposal in the company's format.
  • A material order generated automatically from an accepted estimate and sent to the supplier.
  • A production calendar that schedules crews against material delivery dates and weather holds.
  • Automatic sync of completed jobs into QuickBooks so invoices and deposits are recorded once.
  • A warranty register that records installed roofs and sends the registration paperwork to the manufacturer.
