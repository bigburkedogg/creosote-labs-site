Systems and automation in Kingman

Businesses in Kingman and the rest of Mohave County tend to accumulate tools one at a time: QuickBooks for invoicing, a shared Google Sheet or Excel workbook for scheduling, a paper or PDF work order, and a text thread for field updates. Trucking and distribution companies, HVAC and plumbing contractors, medical offices, and small manufacturers often keep the same customer, job, or order information in 3 or 4 places. Systems and automation work covers the gap between those tools.

Creosote Labs consolidates those spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record, then builds the internal tools around it: a scheduling board, a quoting form, a job tracker, or a reporting page that pulls from live data. Repetitive steps such as re-keying invoices into QuickBooks, sending follow-up emails, and generating work orders are automated. Every project begins with a $3,000 operations review, and each recommendation in the written assessment carries its own fixed quote. We document the finished system and hand it to staff.

Common projects

  • A dispatch board for a Kingman trucking company that replaces a whiteboard and a shared spreadsheet with one schedule drivers can see on their phones.
  • A work order system for an HVAC contractor that creates the invoice in QuickBooks when a technician marks the job complete.
