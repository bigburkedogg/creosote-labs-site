Systems and automation for plumbing companies

Even with a field service platform in place, a plumbing company often keeps parts of the operation elsewhere. Inventory on trucks lives in a Google Sheet, water heater warranty registrations in a binder, service agreement renewals in an Excel file the office manager updates by hand, and permit tracking in email. Each of these takes re-entry when something changes, and none of them talk to ServiceTitan or QuickBooks. Systems and automation work addresses that gap between the platform and everything around it.

Creosote Labs builds the single system of record those pieces belong in and connects it to the tools already running the business. A typical project replaces 3 or 4 spreadsheets with one internal application for truck stock, service agreements, and permit status, with automated reminders when a renewal or inspection comes due. Every project is scoped and quoted from the operations review, documented in writing, and handed to the office staff who will run it. Pricing is fixed, with no hourly billing.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A truck inventory tracker that records parts used per job and generates a restock list for the supply house run each morning.
  • A service agreement database that flags annual water heater flushes and backflow tests coming due and queues the reminder call.
  • An automation that creates the QuickBooks invoice when a job is marked complete in Housecall Pro, with no re-keying by the office.
  • A permit and inspection log tied to each job, with a dashboard showing which jobs are waiting on the city.
  • A callback tracker that records warranty returns by technician and job type for the owner's monthly review.
  • A commercial maintenance schedule for property management accounts, with a report each customer receives after the visit.
