Systems and automation for cleaning companies

A cleaning company with a few crews often runs on a scheduling app such as ZenMaid or Jobber, a QuickBooks file for invoicing, a spreadsheet of client addresses and gate codes, and a group text for the crews. Recurring schedules change when clients skip a week, crews call out, or a commercial account adds a floor. Keeping the calendar, the payroll hours, and the invoices in agreement takes manual work at the end of every day and every pay period.

Creosote Labs consolidates those pieces into one system of record for clients, jobs, crews, and billing. We connect the scheduling tool to QuickBooks so completed jobs become invoices without re-entry, build a crew check-in that records arrival, departure, and checklist completion from a phone, and automate reminders, rescheduling confirmations, and past-due follow-up. Everything is documented and handed over to the office staff who will run it.

What we do

  • Consolidate spreadsheets and disconnected tools into one system of record.
  • Build internal tools for scheduling, quoting, tracking, and reporting.
  • Automate repetitive work such as data entry, follow-up, and document generation.
  • Document the system and hand it over to staff.

Common projects

  • A client database that replaces the address-and-gate-code spreadsheet and feeds the daily route sheet.
  • An integration that turns completed jobs in Jobber or Housecall Pro into QuickBooks invoices the same day.
  • A mobile checklist for crews that records photos and completion times per room.
  • Automated appointment reminders and skip-or-reschedule confirmations sent by text.
  • A payroll hours report built from crew check-ins instead of paper timesheets.
  • A dashboard showing recurring revenue, cancellations, and crew utilization by week.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do we have to leave ZenMaid or Jobber?

    Not necessarily. Where the existing scheduling tool works well, we build around it and connect it to the rest of the business; where it is the source of the re-entry, the operations review will say so and quote a replacement.

  • What happens to the data in our spreadsheets?

    We import it into the new system, clean up duplicates and outdated client records along the way, and verify it with the office staff before anything goes live.
