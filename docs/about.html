Brian Burke builds software and runs it. Before Creosote Labs he was chief operating officer of a venture-backed Silicon Valley startup, where he worked with the company's investors. Before that he taught adults for a decade as a yoga teacher, a personal trainer and a golf coach. He is a licensed real-estate agent, owns and renovates rental property, and has a philosophy degree from the University of San Francisco.

Creosote Labs started in 2026. Its projects include a trip-operations system for a river outfitter, a website for a sexology practice, two products in development, a course builder, two learning games and a set of tools. All of it was built by one person with AI coding tools, and most of it is in use.
