About

Creosote Labs is a research and product group working on applied AI.

We build software and then run it: operations systems inside working businesses, tools that read documents never meant to be read by a machine, and applications that teach. A system that has been live through a busy season behaves differently from one that has been demonstrated, and that difference is most of what we write about.

What we work on

  • Operations systems

    Scheduling, inventory and logistics for businesses whose work is booked months ahead and then executed in the field, where a late change has to reach every downstream list at once.

  • Document tooling

    Pulling structure out of documents laid out for human readers rather than for machines — contracts, agency forms, and packets that arrive as flat PDFs with nothing machine-readable in them.

  • Teaching applications

    Turning a fixed body of source material into something a specific reader can work through, and checking whether they actually did.

  • Deployment and handover

    What a system needs in order to keep running after the people who built it are gone: documentation, defaults, and the failure modes that surface in month three rather than week one.

The systems are on the projects page. What we have published is on the writing page.
