About

01

What we build and run

Creosote Labs is a research organization working on applied AI: what these systems do once they are deployed into a working business. We build software and run it in production: operations systems, document tooling, teaching applications, and assessment-and-course engines for practitioners. We also develop products of our own, including Longtable and Scenario.

02

What we publish

We publish notes on what we build and how it behaves in use, including the parts that did not work. They are on the notes page and in the RSS feed.
